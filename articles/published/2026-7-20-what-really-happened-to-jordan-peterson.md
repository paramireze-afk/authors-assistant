This is the text message Kanye West famously shared from his personal trainer. Kanye famously started crashing out around 2022, claiming that the music industry was controlled by an “Italian” mafia. He wasn’t the first person to make allegations along those lines. Similar claims about organized crime and powerful networks influencing Hollywood and the music industry have been made by other public figures over the years, with Mel Gibson being one of the most well-known examples.

Although their antisemitic remarks were regrettable, I don’t think it makes sense to dismiss every allegation outright because of the offensive things they later said. When multiple people independently describe similar patterns, I think it’s reasonable to examine those claims critically and ask whether there’s any evidence behind them, rather than accepting or rejecting them automatically.

Then there is the suspicious case of Jordan Peterson.

For anyone unfamiliar with his story, Peterson was taken to Russia in early 2020 for an experimental treatment related to benzodiazepine dependence and withdrawal. The treatment reportedly involved placing him in a medically induced coma for several days. He was not told nor was he aware of what happened (see second screenshot)




Yes, the man who became famous writing books about discipline, personal responsibility, and confronting chaos somehow ended up Russia for an experimental treatment???
