The “Hamas-tization” of Political Dissent
A deliberate attempt to shut down discourse
Paul Ramirez
Nov 04, 2025
There seems to be a deliberate effort underway to “Hamas-tize” online critics of Israel, to equate dissent with extremism, as to implement “Hate Speech” laws. Does anyone else see that too?

I don’t like talking about this topic because most people are burnt out on it, and I think that fatigue is by design. A lot of conversations are being deliberately hijacked by professional trolls to “Groyperize” dissenters, turning genuine criticism into something that is distasteful to a casual observer

I first noticed something strange when Candace Owens had Nick Fuentes on her show. The next day, Nick would talk insanely mad shit about Candace, saying some of the nastiest things I’ve ever heard, including about Black people in general. It made no sense unless (upon reflection) he was purely trolling

Later, Tucker Carlson commented in his interview with Candace:

TUCKER CARLSON: He [Fuentes] is clearly part of a campaign to discredit non-crazy right voices. That’s obvious. I’ve been around a long time. I know it when I see it.
CANDACE OWENS: I don’t understand it.
TUCKER CARLSON: Right, I think there’s kind of the angry gay kid thing going on.
CANDACE OWENS: Or it’s what you’re saying, where he’s lying to everybody and pretending things are one way when they’re not.

That exchange did not make sense to me until later. After the Charlie Kirk assassination, Nick went on a podcast tour (PBD, Glenn Greenwald, Infowars, Full Send, Fresh & Fit, and The Deen Show) where he spent a lot of time repeating talking points that I noticed other “independant media” were saying, that Candace is a grifter with no receipts even though she was very careful with her claims (which was obvious to anyone who watched her shows). But yeah, the entire thing reminded me of the coordinated media tactic against Joe Rogan during COVID.

What I think is going on is that the current psy-op aims to eliminate the few remaining major dissident platforms that are openly critical of Israel (seven podcasters in five years.. so to speak) while inserting Nick as the “face” of the dissident right, so that later they can claim there are no more “partners for peace.” when it comes to open discourse and dialog
