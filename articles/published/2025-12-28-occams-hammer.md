Occam’s Hammer
A game of Hammer, Paper, and Razors
Paul Ramirez
Dec 28, 2025





It’s an interesting time to be alive, to say the least. What I find genuinely strange is how publicly impolite it has become to even mention corruption—or to question the motives behind wars overseas. It’s even more taboo to question the official narrative around the Charlie Kirk assassination.

When I was young, I used to watch shows with my parents like Murder, She Wrote, Perry Mason, Matlock, and Columbo. There’s nothing more addictive to me than a good murder mystery.

The entire Charlie Kirk assassination follows what I call Occam’s Hammer, where the public is bludgeoned to death with a narrative until people stop asking questions.

Some of my relatives give me a hard time for not believing in God, although, I still go to church with them, when I happen to visit. I still act and behave as if I were religious, much like what Jordan Peterson would say before he was subverted by daily wire.

What I find interesting, though, about the Charlie Kirk story is that some of the highest-profile figures within Turning Point USA appear to have connections to intelligence agencies and former high-level military members. One media personality who shall not be named (rhymes with Pandace) did several episodes on some of those figures and their connections to military intelligence. She coined the term Operation Mocking Paster, a play on Operation Mockingbird, the CIA program to infiltrate and influence the anti-war and countercultural movements in the 60's.

I happened to be visiting my relatives in Georgia when Charlie Kirk was assassination, and I went to church with my family, and upon reflection, the sermon was very strange. The pastor at their church went to great lengths explaining why “the left” are radical fanatics beyond reproach essentially. Now that I think about it, that is exactly what Charlie Kirk fought against. His whole thing was opening dialog and having discourse. To use reason and logic to help mend the cultural divides.

My question for my relatives, is how would you even know if the highest levels of your own religious institutions have been compromised? Why is it that they are encouraging the opposite of empathy and compassion?

Who knows what is true or what isn’t true, but all I know is that anyone who looks at this story is treated every bit as a conspiracy theorist, anti-vaxxer bigot from years ago, which is so boring. Can the goliaths to be have more interesting psy-ops? As someone who grew up watching murder mysteries, why wouldn’t I be interested in this story? Is it really another case of a political assassination by a lone gunman? Do we really have another 30-6 magic bullet explanation, this time there is a miracle neck that can stop a Mauser 30-6 round? If Angela Lansbury was alive today, she couldn’t dream of writing a better script than the Charlie Kirk story.
