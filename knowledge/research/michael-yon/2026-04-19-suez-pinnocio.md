## The Origins of Israel: Suez Canal, the Jewish Colonial Trust, and the "Pinocchio" Framework

This is the most developed and most significant new material in this transcript.

**Physical artifact discussed:** Yon displays what he describes as an original 1899 share warrant (stamped/issued 1913) from the **Jewish Colonial Trust**, which he states bears Theodor Herzl's name, purchased from an antique dealer in Panama City. He states he separately owns original Herzl books and has "a whole section" of his library devoted to the origins of Israel.

**Yon's core historical claim:** he argues Israel exists for structurally the same reason Panama exists — that "ideology was weaponized to get control of" a critical maritime chokepoint. Specifically, he states the Suez Canal (opened 1869, which he says was called "the spinal cord of the British Empire") was a British strategic asset threatened by potential Egyptian control, and that **British intelligence "used Zionists" — his word, "created" Israel as a "Pinocchio" — to secure control of the canal and surrounding resource-rich territory.** He states explicitly: "This is not conspiracy theory. It's as common in war as bows and arrows and rifles."

**The "Pinocchio" analogy, explained by Yon:** he draws on the original Pinocchio story (referencing his own copy of the book) — a wooden puppet carved to be controlled by strings, which comes to life and (in the version Yon recounts) attacks its own creator first. He applies this directly: a state or political entity created as a controlled instrument ("Pinocchio country," "Pinocchio army") can, in his framework, either be deliberately dismantled once it's no longer useful, or can "grow" past its controllers' intent and turn on them, or in some cases can grow powerful enough to effectively become the new controller of its former masters ("sometimes the boy runs off the strings and actually it just pulls the string master down and now it's the master").

**Yon's explicit prediction, stated to a named associate:** Yon describes a conversation with a friend — described as a former IDF special forces contact he has known for years, met originally in India — in which he told this contact directly that he believes **Israel will be destroyed**, framing this within the "Pinocchio has overstayed its welcome" logic above. He states Israel was "created" by Zionists whom he describes as simultaneously organic and instrumentalized ("they were organic but they were also created"), then "nuclearized" by British intelligence and later brought under US patronage.

**Method of drawing this connection to Green Beret doctrine:** Yon states that raising and later demobilizing foreign proxy forces is a core historical function of US Army Special Forces, citing what he describes as the doctrinal "seven phases of a US-sponsored unconventional war," with the final phase historically called "demobilization," more recently (per Yon) termed "transition" — the point at which a sponsoring power seeks to stand down a force or proxy entity it originally built. He draws a direct parallel between this doctrine and his Pinocchio framing of Israel's founding and (in his prediction) eventual dismantling.

**On defining "Zionist":** when pressed to be precise about who "the Zionist" actor actually refers to, Yon states the term is deliberately hard to pin down, comparing it to the difficulty of precisely defining "Arab" or "Catholic" — categories he argues resist simple definition under scrutiny. He does not offer a working definition of "Zionist" in this transcript beyond the historical Suez/British-intelligence origin story above.

**Historical precedent cited:** Yon references a 1932 book (in his personal library) documenting the legal and "information war" methods used in Greek-Bulgarian-Turkish population exchanges, presenting it as an example of a much older, precedented pattern of population engineering he sees as structurally similar to what he believes is happening currently.

**Current Israeli migration pattern, as Yon's interpretive evidence:** Yon states Israelis are currently relocating in significant numbers to Cyprus, Thailand (specifically naming Koh Tao and Pai), and Argentina/Patagonia. He frames this not as simple flight from danger but as strategically deliberate relocation to geographically/resource-significant locations, consistent with his broader "routes and resources" framework — explicitly speculative in his own framing ("I don't know," "that could be a coincidence," stated twice regarding the Koh Tao and Pai locations specifically).

## Core Analytical Framework

This transcript adds one major structural element to the framework documented in prior entries: an explicit historical-causal account of Israel's origins (the "Pinocchio" framework) and, built directly on top of it, an explicit prediction of Israel's eventual destruction by the same category of actor Yon says created it.

1. **Yon extends his "routes and resources" framework into an explicit theory of state creation itself** — arguing that political/national entities (Israel, Panama, the Gulf states, and by extension the United States as "a child of globalism") are frequently the product of deliberate great-power instrumentalization tied to control of strategic infrastructure, rather than organic national self-determination alone.
2. **The Pinocchio metaphor functions as Yon's model for the lifecycle of such instrumentalized states** — created as a controlled tool, capable of growing beyond original control, and subject to deliberate dismantling once judged to have "overstayed its welcome." Yon applies this explicitly to predict Israel's destruction, a significant escalation from the "the Zionist" actor-label documented in prior entries into a specific claim about the founding and intended endpoint of a specific nation-state.
3. **Yon's own definitional practice is worth noting directly, as he states it himself:** when asked to define "Zionist" precisely, he argues the term resists precise definition (comparing it to "Arab" or "Catholic"), while continuing to use it as a load-bearing causal actor throughout the same conversation. This is a notable tension in his own stated method, documented here descriptively rather than resolved.
4. **Grand strategy is reaffirmed as Yon's primary interpretive lens**, now with a named collaborator (George McMillan) and a specific claim about who occupies this level of understanding ("1% of 1% of 1%").
5. **Yon shows willingness to publicly disagree with a close associate** (David Rogers Webb's "all wars are bankers' wars" claim), which is useful context for calibrating how uniformly Yon accepts claims within his broader social/media network versus applying independent judgment.

One further note on this transcript specifically, offered descriptively and consistent with how this reference set has handled comparable material throughout: the claim that a state was deliberately created as a controlled instrument by a hidden intelligence apparatus, has since grown beyond its creators' control, and is now slated for destruction by those same unnamed controlling interests, is — considered purely as a narrative structure — a version of the same hidden-controller pattern documented in this reference set's Core Analytical Framework sections previously, now applied specifically and explicitly to the founding and predicted end of a named nation-state rather than to an unspecified global actor. This is noted here in the same descriptive spirit as previous entries: not as an evaluation of the claim's truth, but as an accurate description of what kind of claim it structurally is, which this reference aims to represent faithfully rather than sanitize.
