# The Aspen Donor Retreat (August 2025)

## Overview

In mid-August 2025, Turning Point USA held what internal sources described as its “first ever super high level board investor executive retreat” in Aspen, Colorado [3]. The retreat took place approximately August 15-17, 2025, roughly three weeks before Charlie Kirk was assassinated at Utah Valley University on September 10, 2025.

The event is significant to independent investigators for several reasons: Kirk allegedly made a succession statement naming his wife Erika as his replacement; donors raised the topic of what would happen to TPUSA if Kirk died; and the retreat occurred in the middle of a documented pressure campaign by pro-Israel donors who were attempting to reverse Kirk’s shifting foreign policy positions. Multiple attendees have disputed the official account of what was said at the retreat, and the audio evidence Turning Point USA has released to support that account is itself contested.

This document does not assert that anything improper occurred at the Aspen retreat. It organizes what is known, what is disputed, and what remains unanswered.

## Background: Why a Retreat at All?

### The Organizational Context

By summer 2025, Turning Point USA was under internal and external pressure on multiple fronts:

- Kirk had hosted Tucker Carlson, Dave Smith, and Megyn Kelly at the TPUSA Student Action Summit in July 2025, drawing furious backlash from pro-Israel donors [19]
- Kirk was reportedly receiving dozens of hostile texts and calls from Netanyahu-aligned donors [17]
- A separate Hamptons meeting had already occurred in early August [20], where Kirk faced what he privately described as an “intervention” over his Israel positions
- On September 2, just days after the Aspen retreat, Kirk would issue an internal memo removing Tyler Bowyer as COO and requesting a DOGE-style financial audit [23]

Against this backdrop, TPUSA held what Baron Coleman’s investigation describes as an unprecedented executive-level gathering [3]. The timing places it squarely within a documented period of organizational turbulence.

### The Hamptons Meeting vs. The Aspen Retreat

Important distinction: These are two separate events, and sources sometimes conflate them. Investigators should treat them as distinct:

| Feature | Hamptons Meeting | Aspen Retreat |
|---|---|---|
| Approximate date | August 4-5, 2025 | August 15-17, 2025 |
| Primary focus (official) | “Save the West” / Islam | TPUSA multi-year vision |
| Key attendee (confirmed) | Bill Ackman, Seth Dillon, Josh Hammer | Donors, board members, executives |
| Netanyahu phone call | Reported [20] | Not reported |
| Succession discussion | Not reported | Central to controversy |
| Kirk’s characterization | “Intervention” | Not publicly characterized by Kirk |

Some source material uses “Hamptons” and “Aspen” interchangeably or attributes the succession discussion to the Hamptons meeting. Erika Kirk’s own account [8] is ambiguous about which event she is describing. Investigators should note this ambiguity when evaluating claims.

## What Allegedly Happened at Aspen

### The Succession Statement

The central controversy involves a statement Charlie Kirk allegedly made at the Aspen retreat naming Erika Kirk as his successor to lead Turning Point USA.

According to Erika Kirk, speaking in a post-assassination interview, Charlie responded to a donor question about TPUSA’s future by saying the organization was “vision led” rather than “founder led,” and that Erika “would do a great job running it” [8]. Erika later cited this statement publicly, including in a Megyn Kelly interview, as the basis for her assumption of leadership over TPUSA.

Turning Point USA has claimed a video recording of this statement exists. However, the organization has released only audio, not video, despite the event reportedly being recorded [5].

### The Donor Question About Kirk’s Death

Independent investigators flag one detail as particularly unusual: at the retreat, a donor asked a 31-year-old what would happen to his organization if he died [7]. Baron Coleman’s investigation characterizes this as a “morbid and unusual question” for a healthy young man in his early thirties, and raises the possibility that some attendees may have had foreknowledge of threats to Kirk’s life [8].

Evidentiary status: This framing is inferential. The question could reflect standard succession planning concerns common at donor retreats for founder-led organizations. It could also reflect awareness of the documented threat environment Kirk was operating in by August 2025. Both interpretations are consistent with the available evidence.

### The Multi-Year Vision Presentation

Separately from the succession controversy, a TPUSA senior director confirmed in an interview that Kirk used the retreat to lay out a multi-year organizational vision [3]. This is consistent with the retreat’s stated purpose as a high-level strategic gathering. Investigators note the irony that Kirk was presenting a long-term organizational roadmap approximately three weeks before his death.

## The Audio Evidence: Contested and Unexplained

### What TPUSA Released

At AmFest (TPUSA’s annual conference), the organization played audio of Charlie Kirk naming Erika as his successor. No video was shown [5].

### Why Investigators Question the Audio

Multiple independent lines of challenge have emerged:

- Witness testimony: Four attendees of the Aspen donor event were contacted by Candace Owens’ investigation. Three of the four, including one couple, did not remember Charlie making the succession statement [4]. A fourth witness was considered conflicted due to ties to TPUSA beyond a standard donor relationship [4].
- Expanded witness pool: In a later episode, Candace Owens reported that two additional donors came forward to say the statement never occurred, and that Erika was not even present at the main presentation. During a smaller dinner with approximately 12 attendees, Charlie never mentioned Erika in relation to succession [1].
- Audio characteristics: The released audio sounds “abruptly edited,” with the succession statement appearing disconnected from surrounding remarks about the board handling organizational matters [4].
- The video question: Turning Point USA has stated it possesses video of the statement but has declined to release it. Investigators note that releasing such a video would have been a powerful moment at AmFest, and its absence is treated as evidence the footage may not exist [2].
- Internal contradiction: Charlie Kirk had made repeated public statements that women, particularly mothers with young children, should not aspire to corporate leadership roles, positions he maintained even alongside Erika [5]. Investigators argue the alleged succession statement is inconsistent with his documented public philosophy.

Evidentiary status of the audio challenge: The witness testimony is significant but not independently verified. The audio editing claim is an assessment, not a forensic finding. The video non-release is a documented fact. The philosophical inconsistency argument is inferential. Taken together, investigators argue the cumulative weight of these challenges is substantial [2].

## The Pressure Campaign Context

The Aspen retreat did not occur in isolation. It was the second of at least two high-pressure gatherings Kirk attended in August 2025, both connected to his shifting positions on Israel and Netanyahu.

### The Documented Pressure Timeline

By the time of the Aspen retreat, the following had already occurred:

- Kirk hosted Tucker Carlson and Dave Smith at the July Student Action Summit, drawing donor fury [19]
- Netanyahu personally called Kirk during the Hamptons meeting and offered to take TPUSA “to the next level financially” [20]
- Kirk rejected both the funding offer and an invitation to visit Israel [20]
- Kirk vented publicly about being “morally blackmailed” on Megyn Kelly’s show on August 6 [20]
- Infowars host Harrison Smith posted on August 13 that Kirk believed a foreign ally would kill him if he turned against them [10]

The Aspen retreat occurred approximately two days after the Harrison Smith post.

### Robert Shillman’s Withdrawal

Four days before Kirk’s assassination, tech billionaire Robert Shillman cancelled a commemorative plaque at TPUSA’s Phoenix headquarters and terminated his financial support. This was announced at a private American Freedom Alliance dinner in Los Angeles on September 6 [18]. The Aspen retreat preceded this withdrawal by approximately three weeks, and investigators treat Shillman’s action as part of the same escalating pressure sequence.

### The WhatsApp Group Kirk Never Joined

According to Max Blumenthal’s reporting at The Grayzone, Kirk had agreed to join a WhatsApp group organized to coordinate pro-Israel talking points during his campus tour. He was killed before the tour began and never joined the group [18]. The Aspen retreat falls within the period when this coordination was apparently being arranged.

## TPUSA’s Post-Assassination Conduct

Investigators treat TPUSA’s behavior after Kirk’s death as relevant context for evaluating the Aspen retreat’s significance:

- Erika Kirk assumed leadership of TPUSA, citing the alleged Aspen succession statement as her mandate
- TPUSA 2.0 was announced, described internally as “the upgrade,” and donor money reportedly increased [11]
- TPUSA’s foreign policy positioning shifted to become “decidedly pro-Middle Eastern foreign policy engagement,” the opposite direction from where Kirk was heading [11]
- The DOGE-style financial audit Kirk requested on September 2 was cancelled [23]
- The three missing Form 990 filings (Turning Point Endowment, Turning Point Action, America’s Turning Point) were never submitted [22]

Investigators argue this pattern suggests the Aspen retreat, and the succession statement allegedly made there, became the legal and organizational foundation for a post-Kirk TPUSA that moved in the exact direction Kirk had been resisting.

## What Is Well Supported

- A high-level TPUSA donor and executive retreat occurred in Aspen in mid-August 2025, approximately three weeks before Kirk’s assassination. This is confirmed by multiple sources including a TPUSA senior director [3].
- The retreat occurred during a documented period of intense pressure on Kirk from pro-Israel donors, following the Hamptons meeting and preceding Shillman’s withdrawal [17], [18], [20].
- Erika Kirk publicly cited a succession statement allegedly made at this retreat as the basis for her assumption of TPUSA leadership. This is documented through her Megyn Kelly interview and TPUSA’s AmFest audio presentation [5].
- Turning Point USA has released audio but not video of the alleged succession statement, despite claiming video exists [2].
- At least three attendees contacted by independent investigators did not recall the succession statement being made [4].
- Two additional donors came forward separately to say the statement did not occur and that Erika was not present at the main presentation [1].
- A donor at the retreat asked a 31-year-old what would happen to his organization if he died [7].
- Kirk requested a DOGE-style financial audit and removed his COO eight days after the retreat [23].

## What Remains Uncertain

### About the retreat itself

- The precise dates and location within Aspen are not publicly confirmed
- The full attendee list has not been released or independently established
- Whether the retreat was recorded in full, and by whom, is unknown
- Whether any recordings other than the released audio exist is unknown

### About the succession statement

- No independent forensic analysis of the audio has been conducted or published
- The identity of the “conflicted” fourth witness who did recall the statement has not been disclosed [4]
- Whether Erika Kirk was present at the main presentation, the dinner, or both is disputed [1]
- Kirk’s intent in making the statement, if he made it, is unknown

### About the donor question regarding Kirk’s death

- Who asked the question about what would happen if Kirk died has not been identified
- Whether this question was scripted, spontaneous, or part of a standard agenda is unknown

### About the broader pressure campaign

- The precise connection between the Aspen retreat and the Hamptons meeting, in terms of who coordinated them and whether they were related efforts, has not been established
- Whether Netanyahu or his allies had any involvement in organizing the Aspen retreat is not documented

### About TPUSA’s post-assassination conduct

- Whether the cancelled audit and missing Form 990s are connected to the Aspen retreat or to Kirk’s death is not established
- The legal basis for Erika Kirk’s assumption of leadership, beyond the contested audio, has not been made public
