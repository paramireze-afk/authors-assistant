# The Military's AI Leaked Its Own Guest List

## An incredibly embarrassing story you will certainly never hear

Hey guys, a mistake was made recently that would make every IT person cringe, especially when it happens at the highest levels of military intelligence.

First, some background. There's an ongoing controversy over whether Mitch Snow, a former special forces operator, correctly identified key members of Charlie Kirk's security team, as well as Erika Kirk, at Fort Huachuca, the US's premier intelligence base, in the day or days before the assassination.

Why does Fort Huachuca matter? According to researcher Ana Escobar, in the days leading up to the assassination, the base's expected visitor list included a strange mix of people: top military officials, the director of the Mossad, the people who run the HADES program, and key members of Charlie Kirk's security team. That's a weird group to have heading to the same Army base in the same handful of days.

It also raises another question. Why did the HADES jet, a new Army spy plane and the flagship program of the recon task force at Fort Huachuca, fly over the Provo area twice on the day of the assassination, and again during Tyler Robinson's arrest?

So how did Ana figure out who was expected on that base? Here's where it gets crazy. She says the military uses an AI tool to automate the security checks on its high-profile guest lists, and over the past few months she's used that to piece together who was expected at Fort Huachuca, including Erika Kirk.

And how could anyone outside the base see this? The military's AI software (built on Gemini, Google's AI) runs every expected visitor through Google to make sure it isn't hallucinating. Every one of those checks leaves a digital footprint, which is why Ana and other researchers have been seeing these hits on Google Trends. It basically turns into a public guest list.

As an IT guy, this is the part that gets me. The feature meant to make the AI *more* reliable is the thing that leaked the data.

The way I see it, the entire Charlie Kirk assassination has been a complete disaster for those trying to get away with the crime. It sucks that Erika Kirk is implicated, because it sucks that that level of evil can exist. And it sucks that everyday people don't like to believe such evil can exist, and see people like me as the ones causing problems.

What gives me hope is that evil can also be incompetent. Over time, due to the lack of competition, they get lazy and sloppy and make stupid mistakes. In my opinion, this story represents the complete collapse of the old system, and the old system is trying to trick us into believing that they are still in power, that there is a superstructure. I think they are fighting a war over perception and failing, flailing in the dark, aimlessly looking to gain footing.
