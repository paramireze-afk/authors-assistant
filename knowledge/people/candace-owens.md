# Candace Owens

## Overview

Candace Owens is one of the central figures in the Charlie Kirk investigation. According to the material collected so far, she occupies several roles simultaneously:

- Former colleague and ally of Charlie Kirk
- Independent investigator following his death
- Recipient of alleged warnings after the assassination
- Frequent target of criticism from political commentators
- Subject of competing narratives regarding her credibility

Because of this, she appears repeatedly throughout the investigation and intersects with many of the major people and organizations involved.

---

## Relationship with Charlie Kirk

### Daily Wire Split

- Fired from Daily Wire in March 2024.
- Charlie Kirk publicly supported Owens after her dismissal.
- Owens claims this support created additional friction between Kirk and Ben Shapiro.

### Relationship Prior to Charlie's Death

According to collected material:

- Kirk and Owens remained in communication.
- Kirk reportedly discussed religious changes, including growing interest in Catholicism.
- Kirk allegedly wanted Owens involved with Turning Point USA again shortly before his death.

---

## Events Leading Up to Charlie's Death

Reported events include:

- Charlie allegedly expressed frustration with donor pressure.
- Group text messages reportedly discuss distancing himself from the pro-Israel movement.
- Multiple individuals allegedly told Owens that Charlie said:

> "I think they're going to kill me."

According to Owens:

- Multiple sources independently relayed this statement.
- Some reportedly possess written documentation.

---

## Warnings Received After Charlie's Death

According to Owens:

### Andrew Kolvet

Within days of Charlie's death:

- Kolvet allegedly warned Owens:
  - "It was supposed to be you."
- He reportedly advised:
  - Owens
  - Tucker Carlson

to "lay low."

According to Owens, Kolvet never identified the source of this warning.

### Additional Warning

Owens has also stated she received a similar warning from another individual connected to a CIA contractor.

These warnings have become an important recurring topic within the investigation.

---

## Release of Charlie's Text Messages

Timeline:

- Owens released group text messages discussing Charlie's concerns.
- Critics initially accused her of fabricating them.
- Andrew Kolvet later publicly confirmed the messages were authentic.

This sequence is frequently referenced because it significantly affected Owens' credibility among supporters of the investigation.

---

## Ben Shapiro Conflict

The investigation documents an ongoing conflict between Owens and Ben Shapiro.

Topics include:

- Owens' departure from Daily Wire
- Charlie's public support of Owens
- Public criticism exchanged afterward
- Disagreements regarding:
  - TPUSA
  - Charlie Kirk
  - Erika Kirk
  - the investigation itself

Supporters of Owens argue that some public descriptions of her statements mischaracterize what she actually said. Critics dispute that characterization.

---

## Media Response

According to various commentators referenced in the investigation:

- Conservative media figures were encouraged to criticize Owens.
- Mainstream media reportedly amplified criticism of her investigation.
- Owens became one of the primary subjects of public debate surrounding Charlie Kirk's death.

These claims remain part of the broader narrative and are themselves disputed.

---

## Alleged Threats

Owens has publicly stated:

- She received multiple warnings following Charlie's death.
- She believes her life is in danger.
- She has made additional claims involving foreign intelligence and political figures.

These allegations have not all been independently verified within the collected material and should be treated as reported claims.

---

## Israel-Related Reporting

Owens' reporting frequently focuses on:

- Charlie's changing views regarding Israel
- donor influence
- foreign policy
- relationships inside conservative organizations

Her critics often characterize this reporting as conspiratorial, while her supporters argue she is investigating legitimate unanswered questions.

## Observed Patterns

The collected material consistently portrays Owens as:

- a close associate of Charlie Kirk during his final months,
- an important source of information after his death,
- a controversial public figure whose credibility is heavily debated,
- someone whose reporting has generated significant public disagreement.

Many conclusions drawn from Owens' reporting remain contested. Future documentation should continue distinguishing between:

- documented events,
- reported statements,
- independent corroboration,
- and analysis or interpretation.

This distinction will make future research and writing easier while preserving the underlying source material.
