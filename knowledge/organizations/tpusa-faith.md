## 1. What Is TPUSA Faith?

TPUSA Faith is a ministry and outreach division operating under the Turning Point USA umbrella. Its stated mission is to “empower Christians to put their faith into action” through campus chapters, pastor networks, and political engagement framed in explicitly Christian terms.

It is not a separate legal entity in the way that Turning Point Action (the 501(c)(4) political arm) is. It functions as a programmatic division of the parent 501(c)(3) nonprofit, Turning Point USA (EIN 80-0835023).

TPUSA Faith is significant to investigators for several reasons:

- It represents a documented, measurable ideological departure from TPUSA’s founding mission
- Its launch correlates directly with the largest revenue increases in TPUSA’s history
- Its primary architect, Pastor Rob McCoy, is cited repeatedly in independent investigations as a figure of interest
- Its pro-Israel theological framework is central to theories about why Charlie Kirk may have become a liability to certain donors and power structures
- Charlie Kirk himself, in private communications cited by multiple investigators, expressed growing discomfort with the direction TPUSA Faith was taking the organization

## 2. Origins and Founding Context

### The Original TPUSA Mission (2012 to approximately 2020)

Documented fact: Turning Point USA was founded in 2012 by Charlie Kirk, then 18 years old, after being discovered by co-founder Bill Montgomery at Benedictine University. Montgomery reportedly told Kirk not to attend college but instead to build a youth organization [7].

Documented fact: Archive captures of the TPUSA website from March 2019 and July 2021 show no mention of faith, Christianity, God, or Jesus. The organization’s stated mission was to “identify, educate, train, and organize students to promote fiscal responsibility, free markets, and limited government” [5].

Documented fact: Bill Montgomery, TPUSA’s co-founder and Kirk’s primary mentor, died from COVID-19 in July 2020 at age 80, leaving Kirk in sole control of the organization [5].

Analytical observation (Baron Coleman): Montgomery is described as a grounding influence who kept Kirk focused on the economic libertarian mission. His death is identified as the proximate event that allowed the religious pivot to occur [7].

### The Pivot (2020 to 2021)

Documented fact: TPUSA Faith launched in 2020, led by Pastor Rob McCoy of Godspeak Calvary Chapel in Thousand Oaks, California [4].

Official statement (TPUSA): The division was created to “empower Christians to put their faith into action.”

Documented fact: The faith component was apparently in development before the official 2020 launch. Erika Frantzve (later Erika Kirk) was reportedly being interviewed for a role developing a “faith component” within TPUSA as early as August or September 2018, during her first meeting with Charlie Kirk [10].

Analytical observation: This means the religious pivot was being planned at least two years before it publicly launched, during the same period Kirk met his future wife.

## 3. Key People

### Rob McCoy

Role: Founding leader of TPUSA Faith; Senior Pastor of Godspeak Calvary Chapel, Ventura County, California.

Documented fact: McCoy is credited by Kirk himself with transforming TPUSA from a secular organization into one declaring “God was on the side of conservatives” [7].

Documented fact: McCoy’s son and daughter-in-law work for Turning Point USA [9].

Investigative observation (Candace Owens): McCoy’s sermons are described as “as much hour-long doses of Israeli propaganda as they are anything else” [6]. This characterization is the investigator’s framing, not a neutral description.

Investigative hypothesis (Baron Coleman): McCoy is identified as “indirectly, possibly directly, responsible for Kirk’s death” on the theory that the religious pivot attracted donor networks with pro-Israel commitments that Kirk later tried to distance himself from [1]. This is a hypothesis, not a documented fact.

Public controversy: McCoy publicly criticized Candace Owens for investigating Charlie Kirk’s death [9]. The fact of the criticism is documented; its motivation is disputed.

### Erika Frantzve Kirk

Role: Charlie Kirk’s wife; assumed leadership of TPUSA following his assassination on September 10, 2025.

Documented fact: Erika and Charlie Kirk met in August or September 2018. Multiple versions of the meeting story have emerged, shifting from a job interview to a casual visit arranged by Tyler Bowyer to a chance encounter [11].

Documented fact: TPUSA revenue nearly tripled in the fiscal year following their meeting, jumping from approximately $10.8 million to $29 million [3].

Documented fact: Revenue jumped again from $55 million to over $80 million after they married in 2021, coinciding with the full rollout of TPUSA Faith [2].

Official statement (Erika Kirk): On a September 22, 2025 conference call, one day after Charlie Kirk’s memorial, she stated: “We are moving forward with Turning Point 2.0 with Charlie in our hearts” [12].

Investigative hypothesis: Multiple independent investigators treat the revenue correlation, the “2.0” language, and Erika’s rapid organizational pivot as evidence of foreknowledge or planning. This is a hypothesis. The revenue correlation is documented; the interpretation is contested.

Documented fact: Erika Kirk serves on the TPUSA board of directors alongside David Engelhardt, Doug DeGroote, Mike Miller, and Tom Sedaika [15].

### Charlie Kirk

Role: Founder and CEO of TPUSA until his assassination.

Documented fact: Kirk’s salary grew from approximately $80,000 to over $290,000 between 2018 and 2019 [3].

Documented fact (via Candace Owens): In February 2024, Kirk messaged Owens saying “Catholicism is sounding better and better” and they discussed how “Judeo-Christianity seemed more like a cult than theology, particularly regarding political pressures around Israel” [9].

Documented fact (via Candace Owens): A group chat from two days before the assassination shows Kirk was “done with Israel bullying him” and facing pressure from Jewish donors [9]. Note: the existence and content of this group chat is reported by Owens; it has not been independently verified.

Documented fact: Kirk announced a “DOGE” internal audit of TPUSA on September 2, 2025, eight days before he was killed [19].

Investigative hypothesis: Kirk’s private Catholic conversion, his distancing from the Israel framework, and his announcement of an internal audit are treated by investigators as connected events that made him a liability. This is a hypothesis.

### Bill Montgomery

Role: TPUSA co-founder; Kirk’s original mentor.

Documented fact: Montgomery died July 2020 from COVID-19 at age 80 [5].

Documented fact: Montgomery introduced the auditing firm Stapleton Group to TPUSA while simultaneously serving as a “business development advisor” for that same firm, creating a direct independence conflict [23].

Documented fact: An Ohio State accounting professor called the arrangement “a clear conflict” and a former New York assistant attorney general said it “does not pass the smell test” [23].

Analytical observation: Montgomery’s death removed the primary check on Kirk’s organizational direction and opened the door to McCoy’s influence.

## 4. Organizational Structure and Governance

Documented fact: TPUSA Faith operates as a programmatic division of Turning Point USA, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. It is not a separately incorporated entity.

Documented fact: TPUSA is governed by a board of directors. As of late 2025, that board consists of Erika Kirk, David Engelhardt, Doug DeGroote, Mike Miller, and Tom Sedaika [15].

Documented fact: As a 501(c)(3), TPUSA has an absolute legal prohibition on partisan political activity [20].

Documented fact: TPUSA paid approximately $6.6 million total to Rally Forge and its successor entity 1Ten, a firm Facebook permanently banned for coordinated inauthentic behavior on behalf of TPUSA [20] [22].

Documented fact: Jake Hoffman, who ran Rally Forge, was later indicted in Arizona’s alternate electors case alongside TPUSA COO Tyler Bowyer [22].

Documented fact: TPUSA Faith distributed packets in late September 2025 that mentioned Israel eight times [25].

Analytical observation: The distribution of explicitly pro-Israel faith materials within weeks of Kirk’s assassination, by an organization Kirk was reportedly distancing himself from, is noted by investigators as significant. The significance is contested.

### Insider Contractor Network

Documented fact: Five TPUSA insiders ran side companies billing the nonprofit for over $10 million combined [20].

Documented fact: Three shell companies (Lionrock Ventures, Cloverstone Ventures, and GSM Strategy) received $2.7 million and are all linked to Senior Advancement Director Stacy Sherean, who remained on the payroll at over $180,000 per year [21].

Documented fact: On May 15, 2025, TPUSA filed only one of four required Form 990 returns. The forms for Turning Point Endowment, Turning Point Action, and America’s Turning Point were never submitted [26]. This prevented public scrutiny of how $8.6 million sent to America’s Turning Point (a shell company with zero reported employees) was spent.

Analytical observation: The combination of missing financial filings, insider contractor networks, and a pending internal audit creates a documented financial motive for parties who benefited from the existing arrangements. Whether that motive connects to Kirk’s death is a hypothesis.

## 6. Ideological Shift: What Changed and When

### What Changed

Documented fact: TPUSA’s stated mission shifted from fiscal conservatism and limited government to a framework explicitly combining Christian nationalism with strong support for the political state of Israel and Israeli foreign policy [6].

Documented fact: TPUSA Faith distributed materials mentioning Israel eight times in packets nominally about faith and American values [25].

Documented fact (via Candace Owens): Kirk privately described “Judeo-Christianity” as resembling “a cult” in the context of political pressure around Israel [9].

Documented fact (via Candace Owens): A group chat two days before the assassination shows Kirk stating he was “done with Israel bullying him” [9]. Independent verification of this chat has not been publicly established.

### When It Changed

Documented fact: The website shows no faith content through at least July 2021 [5].

Documented fact: TPUSA Faith launched in 2020 [4].

Documented fact: The faith component was reportedly in development as early as 2018 [10].

Documented fact: The revenue inflection point correlates with 2019, the year after Kirk met Erika Frantzve [2].

### What Evidence Investigators Cite

Independent investigators cite the following as evidence the shift was externally driven rather than organically developed:

- The revenue correlation with Erika’s arrival and the religious pivot [2]
- Kirk’s private communications expressing discomfort with the Israel framework [9]
- The “chance encounter” meeting story’s multiple contradictory versions [11]
- Erika’s rapid “TPUSA 2.0” pivot following Kirk’s death [12]
- McCoy’s sermons functioning as pro-Israel political content within a faith wrapper [6]
- The TPUSA Faith packets’ disproportionate Israel emphasis [25]

### Competing Explanations

Official/mainstream explanation: The religious pivot reflects organic growth in Kirk’s personal faith journey and a strategic decision to expand TPUSA’s donor base among evangelical Christians. Revenue growth reflects the organization’s success in reaching a larger audience.

Investigative hypothesis: The pivot was engineered by external actors, possibly including Israeli government-connected networks, to convert TPUSA from a libertarian youth organization into a Christian Zionist political operation. Kirk’s assassination is hypothesized to have occurred when he began resisting this direction.

Where they agree: The pivot happened. Revenue exploded after it. Kirk was privately uncomfortable with the Israel pressure in his final months.

Where they diverge: Whether the pivot was organic or engineered; whether Kirk’s discomfort constitutes a motive for his assassination; whether Erika Kirk was a knowing participant or simply a beneficiary.

## 7. The Israel Question

This section addresses the pro-Israel dimension of TPUSA Faith directly, as it appears repeatedly in independent investigations.

Documented fact: TPUSA Faith, under McCoy’s leadership, incorporated strong support for the political state of Israel as a theological position, framing it as a biblical duty for Christians [8].

Documented fact (via Candace Owens): Owens describes Christian Zionism as “a perversion of scripture used to manipulate evangelicals into supporting Israeli actions regardless of morality” [8]. This is her analytical position, not a neutral description.

Documented fact: Kirk was reportedly moving away from this position in the months before his death, including canceling his show on Salem Media [14].

Investigative hypothesis (multiple investigators): Netanyahu or Israeli government-connected networks offered to take TPUSA “to the next level” financially. Andrew Kolvet allegedly confirmed this offer to Candace Owens. Kirk rejected it. He was subsequently killed. After his death, TPUSA became “decidedly pro-Middle Eastern foreign policy engagement” under Erika Kirk [14].

Evidentiary status of the Netanyahu claim: This is an investigative hypothesis. Kolvet’s alleged confirmation to Owens is reported by Owens; no independent verification has been publicly established. Joe Kent’s congressional investigation is reported to be exploring this angle.

Critical distinction: Investigators who raise the Israel angle are examining the actions of a state government and its alleged political networks. This is distinct from any claim about Jewish people as an ethnic or religious group. That distinction is non-negotiable in responsible investigation.

## 8. Controversies

### The Auditor Conflict

Documented fact: TPUSA’s auditor, the Stapleton Group, was introduced by co-founder Bill Montgomery, who simultaneously served as a business development advisor for that firm [23].

Documented fact: The auditor’s firm license had expired [23].

Documented fact: Independent accounting and legal experts called the arrangement a “clear conflict” that “does not pass the smell test” [23].

### Rally Forge and the Troll Farm

Documented fact: Facebook permanently banned Rally Forge in October 2020 for coordinated inauthentic behavior on behalf of TPUSA, involving approximately 200 fake Facebook accounts, 76 fake Instagram accounts, and 55 pages [22].

Documented fact: Rally Forge employed teenagers, including minors, to post fake social media messages without disclosing TPUSA Action as the paying entity [22].

Documented fact: TPUSA paid Rally Forge approximately $1 million over 2018 to 2019 [22].

Documented fact: Hoffman rebranded as 1Ten and was indicted in Arizona’s alternate electors case alongside TPUSA COO Tyler Bowyer [22].

Documented fact: TPUSA paid $6.6 million total to Rally Forge and 1Ten [20], all from a 501(c)(3) with an absolute prohibition on political activity.

### The Missing 990 Filings

Documented fact: Three of four required Form 990 returns for fiscal year 2024 were never filed [26].

Documented fact: This prevented public scrutiny of $8.6 million sent to America’s Turning Point, a shell company with zero reported employees [26].

Documented fact: This occurred four months before Kirk announced his internal audit.

### Erika Kirk’s “TPUSA 2.0” Statement

Documented fact: On September 22, 2025, one day after Kirk’s memorial, Erika Kirk stated on a conference call: “We are moving forward with Turning Point 2.0 with Charlie in our hearts” [12].

Documented fact: She also stated TPUSA would become “the behemoth that he wanted it to be” [13].

Investigative hypothesis: Investigators note that Kirk had reportedly rejected an offer to take TPUSA “to the next level” in August 2025, making Erika’s “behemoth” language appear to contradict his actual wishes [13].

Documented fact: Following Kirk’s death, TPUSA’s public positioning became “decidedly pro-Middle Eastern foreign policy engagement,” the opposite of the direction Kirk was reportedly heading [14].

## 11. Organizational Evolution

TPUSA’s evolution from a secular libertarian youth organization to a Christian Zionist political operation represents one of the more dramatic ideological transformations in modern American conservative politics. The trajectory can be divided into three phases:

- **Phase 1 (2012 to 2018): Secular Economic Libertarianism** Founded on Von Mises Institute-style principles, TPUSA under Kirk and Montgomery was explicitly non-religious. Revenue was modest but growing. The organization’s identity was tied to free markets and limited government.
- **Phase 2 (2018 to 2021): Transition Period** Kirk meets Erika Frantzve. Montgomery dies. McCoy enters. Revenue explodes. The faith component moves from background planning to official launch. The organization’s public identity begins incorporating Christian nationalism.
- **Phase 3 (2021 to present): Christian Zionist Political Operation** TPUSA Faith is fully operational. Revenue reaches $80 to $85 million annually. The organization’s primary public identity is now religious and pro-Israel. Kirk privately expresses discomfort. Kirk announces an internal audit. Kirk is killed. Erika Kirk announces “TPUSA 2.0.” The organization’s post-assassination positioning is described by investigators as more aggressively pro-Israel than at any prior point.

The governance structure is worth noting: because TPUSA is a 501(c)(3) controlled by a board of directors, Kirk never “owned” it in any transferable sense. The board, which now includes Erika Kirk, controls all decisions. This means the organization’s direction after Kirk’s death was determined not by his wishes but by whoever controls the board.
