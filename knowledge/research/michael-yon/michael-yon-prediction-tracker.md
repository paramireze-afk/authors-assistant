# Michael Yon: Prediction Tracking Log

*Purpose: track Michael Yon's specific, checkable claims over time to assess predictive accuracy independent of felt confidence. This log is a companion to the source-documentation files (worldview reference, Morocco/phosphate reference) — those files record what he argues; this file records what he predicts and whether it happened.*

## How to Use This Log

For each entry:
- **Lock the claim's wording before it resolves.** Copy his exact phrasing (or close to it) at the time it's logged, so later assessment isn't influenced by how things actually turned out.
- **Rate specificity honestly.** A claim naming a place, a mechanism, and rough timing is stronger evidence either way than a general mood ("this will get worse").
- **Update status as new information comes in**, and note the date you updated it.
- **Resist counting partial/ambiguous outcomes as clean hits.** If it's arguable, log it as "Ambiguous" rather than rounding up to "Confirmed" — the log is only useful if it resists the same confirmation-bias pull as unaided memory does.
- **Track misses and non-events, not just hits.** A prediction that quietly fails to happen is data. Don't let the log only grow on the "confirmed" side.

**Specificity scale:**
- **High** — names a place/actor/mechanism and a timeframe or clear falsifiable condition.
- **Medium** — names a place/actor but timeframe or mechanism is vague.
- **Low** — general directional claim ("conflict will continue," "tensions will grow") compatible with a wide range of outcomes.

**Status options:** Open · Confirmed · Missed · Ambiguous · Superseded
