# ATF Report and Forensic Findings

## Document Purpose
Canonical reference for all official forensic findings in the Charlie Kirk assassination case,
including ATF ballistics analysis, DNA evidence, and related evidentiary gaps. Covers both
what the official record contains and what it conspicuously omits.

## Status of the ATF Report

The ATF released a summary report in the Charlie Kirk shooting case. Key findings and
notable absences:

### What the Report Confirmed
- The cartridge case (shell casing) recovered at the scene **matched** Tyler Robinson's
  rifle (a Mauser Model 98, .30-06 caliber).
- A bullet jacket fragment was recovered from Charlie Kirk's body, tentatively classified
  as .30-caliber.
- Four separate lead bullet fragments were also recovered from Kirk's body.
- Five additional **unidentified sets of fingerprints** were found on the weapon beyond
  Robinson's.

### What the Report Did NOT Confirm
- The ATF **could not conclusively match** the bullet jacket fragment to Robinson's
  specific rifle.
- The report **did not confirm the rifle was fired on September 10, 2025**: bomb-sniffing
  dogs detected no recent discharge from the weapon. Canines can detect gun residue up to
  seven days after firing, making this a significant evidentiary gap.
- No ballistics report has been publicly released by authorities. The defense confirmed
  in court filings that no ballistics report was turned over in discovery.

## The Bullet-to-Rifle Mismatch

Court filings by Tyler Robinson's defense attorneys revealed that the ATF was **unable to
match the bullet recovered at Kirk's autopsy to Robinson's rifle**. The defense stated its
intention to present the ATF firearms analyst's testimony as **exculpatory evidence**.

This finding is central to the defense's argument that Robinson is a patsy. The cartridge
case match (brass casing) is a much weaker link than a bullet match: casings are ejected
and can be planted; the bullet is what actually traveled through the victim's body.

Sources: Baron Coleman Ep. 89 [ref: tl_bb661049], Candace Owens Ep. 319

## Fragmentation Anomaly

The ATF report's finding of a bullet jacket fragment plus four lead fragments inside Kirk
is itself argued to **undermine** the prosecution's case rather than support it:

- Standard full metal jacket .30-06 rounds do not fragment in soft tissue (neck). They
  pass through cleanly.
- .30-06 rounds fragment primarily upon striking **dense bone** such as a femur or
  shoulder blade.
- Fragmentation in a neck wound is described by independent investigators as "highly
  unusual and potentially devastating to the prosecution's case."
- The fragment pattern is more consistent with a frangible or specialty round, or with
  a device that fragments by design (see: shaped charge theory).

Source: Baron Coleman Ep. 90 (Is TPUSA Violating Judge's Order)

## Comparative Bullet Lead Analysis (CBLA): Discredited Methodology

When standard ballistic matching failed, the FBI indicated it would pursue
**Comparative Bullet Lead Analysis (CBLA)**:

- CBLA was first developed after the JFK assassination in 1963.
- The **National Academy of Sciences declared it unreliable and potentially misleading**
  in 2004.
- The **FBI formally abandoned CBLA in 2005**.
- The ATF characterized current results as **inconclusive** before the technique was
  proposed.
- Independent investigators characterize the revival of CBLA as "an attempt to
  manufacture a case using discredited forensic tactics."

The use of a methodology the FBI itself abandoned two decades ago, in a case where
standard matching failed, is treated by investigators as a significant red flag about
the strength of the underlying evidence.

Source: Candace Owens Ep. 320 (Friends In High Temples)

## DNA Evidence

### What Is Claimed
- The official narrative states Robinson's DNA was found on the rifle trigger.
- The PCA (Probable Cause Affidavit) cites DNA as a key link between Robinson and the
  weapon.

### What Is Not Independently Verified
- No independent lab report is publicly available.
- DNA findings are **state-reported only**: no outside forensic laboratory has publicly
  confirmed the results.
- This means DNA evidence should be treated as a **prosecution claim**, not an
  established fact.

### Complicating Factor: Multiple DNA Profiles
Defense court filings note that **multiple different DNA profiles** were found on items
of evidence. The defense is seeking forensic biologists, geneticists, and statisticians
to evaluate whether the FBI and ATF applied correct scientific procedures in interpreting
mixed DNA profiles.

Mixed DNA profiles are among the most frequently misinterpreted evidence types in
forensic science. The presence of multiple profiles on the rifle is consistent with the
five unidentified fingerprint sets also found on the weapon.

Source: Baron Coleman Ep. 89

## Discovery Volume and Defense Preparation

- The defense has received approximately **20,000 electronic files** as discovery.
- Defense attorneys filed for at least a **six-month delay** of the preliminary hearing
  to properly analyze the scientific reports.
- The government has **not turned over the gun** itself to the defense as of reporting
  through April 2026.
- No video footage of the actual shooting moment has been claimed in any prosecution
  pleading.

Source: Baron Coleman Ep. 106 (Stop Gaslighting the World)

## No Autopsy

A formal independent autopsy was **not performed** on Charlie Kirk. This is a foundational
gap in the evidentiary record:

- All wound descriptions derive from treating physicians and prosecution-sourced
  characterizations, not an independent forensic pathologist's report.
- Utah's Chief Medical Examiner, Deirdre Amaro (quadruple-board certified, appointed
  July 2024), was searched from an Israeli IP address on June 30, 2025, approximately
  ten weeks before the assassination. [Source 5]
- The absence of an independent autopsy means the wound trajectory claim (bullet
  entering front of neck, reversing direction, traveling down through C6, lodging near
  heart) cannot be independently verified.

## Prosecution Evidence Summary (Per Defense Characterization)

Baron Coleman (Ep. 101) characterized the prosecution's physical evidence as:

- Screenshot text messages (no original phone, no forensic extraction)
- A recreated confession note (no original note)
- Shell casings (matched to rifle, not to the bullet in Kirk's body)
- No ballistics report turned over to defense
- No video of the shooting moment in any pleading

The defense has explicitly framed this as "the anatomy of a setup."
