# Charlie Kirk

## Overview

Charles James Kirk, founder and CEO of Turning Point USA (TPUSA), was assassinated on September 10, 2025, at age 31. He was shot while speaking at a public event organized by the UVU chapter of his organization at Utah Valley University in Orem, Utah. He was struck in the neck at approximately 12:23 PM MDT and was declared deceased at Timpanogos Regional Hospital at approximately 2:40 PM.

He was 31 years old.

## Who He Was

Kirk co-founded Turning Point USA in 2012. The organization became one of the most prominent conservative youth activist groups in the United States, known for campus speaking tours, debate events, and a large social media presence. Kirk was a close ally of Donald Trump and a prominent voice in the MAGA media ecosystem.

By the summer of 2025, something was shifting. Kirk had become increasingly uncomfortable with the pro-Israel donor class that had long funded TPUSA. He was reportedly in conflict with major donors over his refusal to distance himself from Tucker Carlson and Candace Owens, both of whom had been critical of Israeli foreign policy. The tension came to a head in the weeks before his death.

## The Final Days

### The Hamptons Retreat (August 5, 2025)

A retreat took place at the Hamptons where Kirk was allegedly pressured by donors. Two sources confirm that Benjamin Netanyahu called Kirk at this dinner and offered to take TPUSA "to the next level" financially. Kirk declined. An army of influencers was subsequently deployed claiming the dinner never happened.

On August 6, the day after the retreat, Kirk appeared visibly irate on Megyn Kelly's show.

### Harrison Smith Warning (August 13, 2025)

Approximately two weeks before the assassination, Harrison Smith tweeted that a friend of Kirk had told him Kirk believed Israel would kill him if he turned against them. No law enforcement agency contacted Smith until public pressure many months after the event.

### The $3.25 Million Contract (August 25, 2025)

A significant financial contract was signed on this date. The same day, TPUSA security planning reportedly began.

### The Donor Withdrawal and the Text (September 8, 2025)

A $2 million donation was pulled from TPUSA. On the same day, Kirk sent a group text to TPUSA employees stating: **"I have no choice but to leave the pro-Israel cause."** Andrew Kolvet later confirmed the authenticity of this text to Candace Owens: "that was a text grab, a screen grab that I had shared with people. So it is authentic."

Kirk also reportedly stated in this period that he would not cancel Tucker Carlson and was considering bringing Candace Owens to the next event.

### "I Think They're Going to Kill Me" (September 9, 2025)

The day before his death, Kirk told at least three different people "I think they're going to kill me." Two sources have this in writing. Erika Kirk initially denied this text existed on the Glenn Beck show, claiming she had Charlie's phone and it never happened. She then claimed it was on Telegram with self-deleting messages. A source confirmed to Candace Owens that it was sent via iMessage. Kolvet confirmed the text's existence.

The sequence is: Kirk leaves the pro-Israel cause, predicts his own death, and is dead within 24 to 48 hours.

### September 9: A Packed Calendar

Kirk's September 9 calendar was reportedly packed: Fox News appearances, driving, prepping with Frank Turek. Two hours before the "kill me" text, Kirk participated in a Zoom call with Josh Hammer and an Israel-based rabbi. Josh Hammer retweeted a Trump post about public executions at 9:05 PM Eastern, approximately 16 hours before Kirk's death.

On the same day, Charlie Kirk LLC (formed in Delaware in May 2018) had its registered address changed back to a UPS store P.O. box, away from TPUSA headquarters where it had been since 2023. This change occurred the day before his death.

### The Fort Huachuca Meeting (September 9, 2025)

Military veteran Mitch Snow accidentally entered a meeting at Fort Huachuca SCIF on September 9. He observed approximately 12 to 13 lieutenant colonels and identified Brian Harpole (Kirk's security chief) and Congressman Mark Amodei (95% certainty). Snow was detained for over 7 hours. A police report with a case number and his son's testimony verify the detention. A SAM/VIP C-37A Gulfstream flew from Colorado Springs to Fort Huachuca on September 9 with its transponder off.

### The Morning of September 10

Kirk flew from Scottsdale to Salt Lake City to speak at the Restaurantology Summit by Savory. He arrived at approximately 9:55 AM, with his session starting at 10:30 AM. He remained on stage until 11:21 AM, then departed for UVU, a 40-minute drive. This schedule meant he would arrive around 12:01 PM for a noon event, leaving him less than 25 minutes before he was shot. The tight schedule gave Kirk minimal time to inspect the setup, notice irregularities (the tent was elevated off the ground on coolers, unusual compared to his typical events), or ask questions about equipment differences.

He had three security personnel with him: believed to be Mikey McCoy, Dan Flood, and a fundraiser.

## The Shooting

At approximately 12:23 PM, while answering a question about mass shootings by transgender individuals, a shot rang out. Kirk was struck in the neck and slumped to the ground almost immediately. He was rushed to Timpanogos Regional Hospital in Orem and declared deceased at approximately 2:40 PM.

His body was removed from the hospital in a motorcade of 8 to 10 vehicles shortly after 8:00 PM, transported to Salt Lake City. An autopsy was reportedly completed sometime between 8:00 PM that night and 2:00 PM the following day, when the body departed on Air Force Two back to Arizona. The body left Utah jurisdiction at 2:00 PM on September 11, ending Utah's investigative access.

No independent autopsy has been publicly confirmed.

## The Wound

The official account states Kirk was struck by a single .30-06 Springfield round fired from a Mauser Model 98 at approximately 160 yards. The bullet allegedly entered the front lower-left neck, struck C2 at the skull base, reversed direction, traveled down through C6, and lodged near the heart, with no exit wound.

Andrew Kolvet claimed, approximately 10 days after the shooting and only after public scrutiny, that the surgeon told him: "I've seen wounds from this caliber many times and they always just go through everything. This would have taken a moose or two down." He called it a "miracle" the bullet stopped. This is Kolvet's characterization of what the surgeon said. No direct surgeon statement is on public record. No ballistics report has been released.

The .30-06 Springfield cartridge generates over 2,500 foot-pounds of energy at the muzzle, is designed for large game, and typically produces catastrophic exit wounds. The absence of an exit wound is considered by independent analysts to be close to a physical impossibility at the claimed range and caliber.

An alternative theory holds that Kirk was killed by a miniaturized shaped charge concealed in his lapel microphone, not a rifle shot. See: **[Shaped Charge Theory]** (separate document recommended).

## Kirk's Relationship with Israel

This is one of the most consequential threads in the entire investigation.

Kirk had long been a prominent pro-Israel voice. Major donors, including Bob Shillman, had funded TPUSA substantially on the basis of that alignment. By summer 2025, Kirk was pulling away. The sequence of events in the final two weeks of his life, from the Hamptons pressure campaign to the donor withdrawal to the "no choice but to leave the pro-Israel cause" text to the death prediction, forms a pattern that independent investigators consider among the most significant evidence in the case.

Post-death, TPUSA raised a quarter-billion dollars, announced "TPUSA 2.0," and shifted toward pro-Israel engagement, the opposite of the direction Kirk had been heading. Erika Kirk became CEO and chairwoman within 72 hours of his death.

## Kirk's Inner Circle (on the day)

- **Mikey McCoy**: TPUSA staffer, reportedly had his phone to his ear less than a second after the shot and calmly walked off.
- **Dan Flood**: Security. A gesture 13 seconds before the shot has been interpreted by some investigators as a possible signal. Significance is disputed.
- **Brian Harpole**: Security chief. Had flown Tokyo to LA to Dallas on September 7 to 8. Identified at Fort Huachuca on September 9. Never walked the UVU campus before the event. Asked UVU Police Chief Jeff Long about rooftop security; Long said "I got you covered." No verification was performed.
- **Andrew Kolvet**: TPUSA spokesperson. Made the "superman neck" claims. Confirmed Kirk's "they're going to kill me" text AND the pro-Israel departure text. Message records show coordination with Erika while she claimed he acted independently.
- **Cooper Brown**: Leadership Institute volunteer who held Kirk's microphone during the event. Unverifiable background. Appeared on Fox News multiple times the same night despite a supposed media blackout for witnesses. Silenced TPUSA student Jeb Jacobi on camera at 12:41 PM.

## What Happened After

- Crime scene paved over 4 days later, on a Sunday in a highly religious state. Paving would eliminate any explosive residue from the scene.
- The SUV that transported Kirk was quickly prepared for auction with no standard insurance documentation. Six interior photos exist showing blood and black plastic fragments.
- Surveillance footage of Robinson surrendering at Washington County Sheriff's Office was declared "unavailable," with a 30-day retention period cited.
- Camera and 4K footage from the event was seized by TPUSA, not by law enforcement. UVU denied all footage requests.
- No ambulance was on standby at the event, despite Utah law requiring one.
- None of Kirk's TPUSA team (Blake Neff, Andrew Kolvet, Mikey McCoy, Terryl Farnsworth) made any 911 calls after Kirk was shot. Brian Harpole claims one call was made en route to the hospital.

## Erika Kirk (née Frantzve)

Kirk's wife. Her parents have backgrounds in military and intelligence. She did not bring family members to Utah; she was accompanied by TPUSA fundraising chief Stacy Sheridan. She became CEO and chairwoman of TPUSA within 72 hours of Kirk's death.

She lied on at least two documented occasions regarding the "I think they're going to kill me" text: first denying it existed, then claiming it was on Telegram with self-deleting messages. A source confirmed to Candace Owens it was sent via iMessage.

Tucker Carlson confirmed on air that Egyptian-registered aircraft followed Erika Kirk to multiple locations worldwide. Baron Coleman documented 73 location overlaps between the SU-BTT transponder data and Erika Kirk's known movements.
