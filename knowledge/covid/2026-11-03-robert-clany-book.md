# Covid and the Corruption of Medical Science

### The arguments of Professor Robert Clancy, as set out in fourteen essays (2021–2026)

> **Note on scope:** This article lays out the arguments and claims made in the book. Statements are the author's positions unless marked *Context*, which indicates extrapolation added to clarify how an argument fits together or what it implies. A short section at the end flags where these claims are disputed.

---

## Overview

Robert Clancy is an emeritus clinical immunologist who spent roughly half a century studying airway and mucosal immunity. His book collects the essays he wrote for *Quadrant* as the pandemic unfolded, deliberately left in chronological order with new preambles describing the situation at the time each was written. The publisher's pitch is that this is a real-time record, not hindsight.

Clancy did not begin as a vaccine opponent. His first essay supported vaccinating Australians while questioning what the new vaccines could realistically achieve. Over five years his position hardens, and the book's overall case becomes institutional as much as scientific: that Australia traded its own pandemic expertise for a narrative shaped by commercial and political interests, largely from the United States.

*Context:* The chronological format matters to the argument. Clancy's credibility claim rests on having predicted outcomes early, so the book is structured to let readers check his forecasts against what followed.

---

## Part 1: The scientific framework

### Two immune systems, not one

The foundation of every chapter is a distinction between:

| | **Systemic immunity** | **Mucosal immunity** |
|---|---|---|
| Where it acts | Bloodstream and tissues | Lining of airways and gut |
| Main antibody | IgG | (Secretory) IgA |
| Stimulated by | Injected vaccines | Infection at the surface; oral/mucosal exposure |
| Works best against | Pathogens that travel through blood (measles, polio) | Pathogens that live on surfaces (influenza, Covid) |

Clancy's argument: vaccines against measles or polio can give "sterilising" immunity and herd immunity because the virus must pass through the blood, where IgG waits. Covid, like influenza, sets up on the airway lining. Blood antibodies only engage once the virus escapes into the lungs and bloodstream, which is the severe-disease stage.

*Context:* The implication is that an injected vaccine against an airway virus can be expected to blunt severity while doing little about catching or spreading the infection. On this view, mandates justified by "protecting others" were built on a misunderstanding of what the product could do.

### The influenza model and the December 2020 predictions

Drawing on roughly 80 years of influenza vaccine experience, Clancy predicted the Covid vaccines would:

1. Reduce severe disease but do little to prevent infection or transmission
2. Provide short-lived protection, weaker in the elderly
3. Require repeated reformulation as the virus mutated
4. Never achieve herd immunity

The summary's author considers these predictions validated by events.

### Immune tolerance

A second idea grows in importance through the book. The mucosal immune system contains strong suppressor ("T regulatory") cells that stop the body overreacting to the enormous microbial load on mucosal surfaces. Repeated exposure to the same antigen strengthens that suppression, which is the principle behind allergy desensitisation shots.

Clancy's claims:

- Closely spaced boosters, combined with natural infection, push populations toward **immune tolerance**, where vaccination is followed by *more* infection, not less.
- The rise in **IgG4** antibodies after repeated mRNA doses is a marker of this tolerance.
- The problem is worse with mRNA vaccines because the amount of spike protein the body produces **cannot be controlled**, unlike a measured protein dose.

*Context:* This is the mechanism that links the book's early and late chapters. It is offered to explain waning effectiveness, the "pandemic of the vaccinated" framing, and later the suggestion of broad "bystander" immune suppression.

## Part 2: The essays in sequence

### January 2021 — "A Realistic Approach to Community Management"
- Proposes a **four-pillar plan**: public health measures, vaccination, early (pre-hospital) treatment, and hospital care.
- Warns against "vaccine nirvana alongside therapeutic nihilism", i.e. pinning everything on vaccines while neglecting treatment.
- Questions the 90%+ efficacy figures, citing Peter Doshi's analysis of FDA data and short trial follow-up.
- Prefers traditional protein-based vaccines such as Novavax.
- Presents evidence he considered supportive of hydroxychloroquine (HCQ) and ivermectin (IVM) as early treatments.
- Reports that after hostile reactions from politicians and the ABC, his university issued a statement that he was not a Covid expert.

### July 2021 — "Where Are We At?"
- Offers a five-step plan, still including 80% vaccination.
- Raises safety concerns: VAERS death reports for mRNA vaccines far above the level that led to withdrawal of the 1976 swine flu vaccine; possible harm from spike protein spreading through the body.
- Argues people recovered from Covid did not need vaccination.
- Calls for local Australian control of vaccine production.

### October 2021 — "Covid Strategy Beyond Lockdown"
- Compares the UK, Israel, Sweden and Iceland.
- Concludes lockdowns only delay infection, and that Australia's "Covid-free" approach left the population immunologically naive.
- Questions vaccinating children; raises myocarditis risk in young males.

*Context:* "Immunologically naive" means Australia entered the vaccine era with little prior infection-derived immunity, which on Clancy's model would make vaccine-only protection more fragile once borders opened.

### December 2021 — "We Can't Vaccinate This Pandemic Away"
- Cites a report of about 600 Australian patients treated by frontline doctors with an ivermectin combination, with no deaths.
- Attacks the TGA's restriction of ivermectin prescribing and AHPRA's warnings to doctors.
- Contrasts this with the government's rapid purchase of Merck's molnupiravir.

### August 2022 — "The Suppression of Useful Covid-19 Treatments"
- Compares patented antivirals (molnupiravir, Paxlovid, remdesivir) with HCQ and ivermectin, concluding the older drugs have better evidence and safety.
- Offers a detailed critique of the TOGETHER ivermectin trial published in the *New England Journal of Medicine*.

### November 2022 — "The Problem with the Covid Narrative"
- Describes professional bodies' silence: the Society for Mucosal Immunology (which he co-founded, with Pfizer as "gold sponsor") did not respond to him; the College of Pathologists declined his proposed national post-mortem study and referred him to the TGA.
- Develops the tolerance argument; describes Covid as having become "a pandemic of the vaccinated."

### May 2023 — "The Curious Tale of Hydroxychloroquine"
- Uses HCQ as a case study of what went wrong.
- Says he wrote around 20,000 HCQ prescriptions in his career without a major side effect.
- Traces the drug's discrediting: the Raoult and Zelenko studies, its association with Trump, the retracted Surgisphere paper, and large trials in **hospitalised** patients, which he argues was the wrong population because antivirals must be given early.
- Argues the WHO's handling of HCQ was a "rehearsal" for centralising control over medical decisions.

*Context:* The "timing" argument is central to his treatment case: if a drug only works early, trials in late-stage patients would be expected to show no benefit regardless of the drug's merit. He extends this into a claim that such trials were "designed to fail."

### June 2024 — "Ivermectin and the Cure for Long Covid"
- Criticises the parliamentary Long Covid inquiry for listing symptoms without investigating causes.
- Drawing on his group's earlier work on fatigue in elite athletes, proposes Long Covid belongs to the **chronic fatigue syndrome** family.
- Model: a genetically predisposed person fails to clear an antigen (here, persistent spike protein), driving chronic inflammation and fatigue.

### November 2024 — "Covid and the Sounds of Silence"
Lists episodes he regards as suppression of legitimate debate:
- A journal debate on HCQ
- A chapter he wrote for a NSW Treasury anniversary book, which was dropped
- Former NSW Premier Dominic Perrottet's admission that vaccine mandates were a mistake
- Construction of an mRNA factory at Macquarie University
- Professor Nikolai Petrovsky's Australian protein-based vaccine being sidelined
- The Senate excess-deaths inquiry, which he says published only 27% of submissions

Proposes an independent Australian advisory group, a revived pandemic plan, and a full inquiry.

### March 2025 — "The Problem That Won't Go Away"
A five-year report card:
- Cites Quebec data showing protection against hospitalisation falling from **93% (2021)** to **54% (XBB booster)**, **23% (JN.1)** and **0% (KP variants)**. He reads this as tolerance plus growing mismatch between vaccine and circulating strains.
- Raises fertility concerns, citing a Czech study and animal data.
- Argues Sweden outperformed Australia on infections, deaths and the economy.
- Calls for a pause in mRNA vaccine use, pointing to Moderna's paused infant RSV trial.

### September 2025 — "Vaccines Ain't Vaccines"
- **Defends traditional childhood vaccines**: disappointment with mRNA Covid vaccines must not undermine confidence in the 27 proven protein-based vaccines on the schedule. Recalls children dying of measles and whooping cough in his youth.
- Floats the idea that repeated boosters may cause broad "bystander" immune suppression, citing:
  - The Cleveland Clinic's reported 27% higher influenza rate among Covid-vaccinated staff
  - A Swiss study of respiratory infections
  - Australia's record whooping cough numbers in 2024

*Context:* This chapter is notable because it separates Clancy from blanket anti-vaccine positions. His objection is framed as specific to mRNA technology and repeated dosing against airway viruses, not to vaccination as such.

### January 2026 — "Post Covid Vaccination Syndrome: The Flagship of Covid"
- Argues post-vaccine syndrome, once estimated at 0.003%, affects around **12%** of vaccinees, based on a German survey of 4,632 people.
- Estimates about **1 million** Australians affected, costing about **$10 billion a year**.
- Describes three "waves" of harm:
  1. Early organ damage from spike protein toxicity and the T-cell response
  2. Chronic systemic inflammation with energy-activated fatigue
  3. Possible genetic effects linked to DNA contamination, reverse transcription and ribosomal "frameshifting"
- Reports treating 25 early patients with ivermectin (which he says binds spike protein); of seven who completed symptom scoring, five improved by more than 50%.
- Calls for large randomised trials.

### May 2026 — "Why WHO?"
- Traces Australia's history of independent pandemic leadership: John Ashburton Thompson and the 1900 plague, Cumpston, CSL, Burnet, and the 2020 national plan.
- Argues the College of Physicians has decayed, leaving a leadership vacuum.
- Criticises the WHO's funding ties to Gates-linked bodies (CEPI, GAVI) and Moderna, and Jane Halton's roles in CEPI and COVAX.
- Urges Australia not to sign the WHO International Health Regulations amendments, framed as a sovereignty issue, and advises the new Australian CDC to stay independent.

### June 2026 — "Not All Excess Pandemic Deaths Were Caused by Covid" (with Clare Pain)
- Argues the Australian Bureau of Statistics changed its baseline in ways that lowered its excess-death estimate.
- Argues Covid deaths were inflated by counting deaths "with" Covid, PCR false positives, and some influenza deaths recorded as Covid.
- Uses Andrew Madry's model (~52,000 excess deaths, 2020–2025), subtracts ~29,000 Covid deaths, and identifies **~23,000 unexplained deaths**.
- Cites: Wilson Sy's reported correlation between vaccine doses and excess deaths ~21 weeks later; Queensland excess deaths beginning before Covid arrived; Japanese data covering 18–21 million vaccination records; and TGA adverse-event data showing more death reports for Covid vaccines than all other vaccines combined.
- Contrasts this with the TGA accepting only 14 vaccine-linked deaths, one after an mRNA vaccine.

*Context:* Note the internal tension the authors must manage: if Covid deaths were *inflated*, the "unexplained" residual grows; the argument therefore depends both on the excess-death baseline and on how many Covid-attributed deaths are discounted.

## Part 3: The case against institutions

Clancy's charge that medical science was "corrupted" rests on several recurring claims:

- **Pharmaceutical influence on evidence.** Vaccine studies were short and observational, capturing a "healthy vaccinee" bias (healthier people are more likely to get vaccinated, flattering outcomes). Trials of cheap repurposed drugs enrolled late-stage hospital patients and were "designed to fail."
- **Journals.** *The Lancet* and *NEJM* published flawed pro-narrative work and refused to engage critics.
- **Media and platforms.** Fact-checkers, social media and legacy press, especially the ABC, enforced orthodoxy.
- **Regulators.** Off-label prescribing was banned and doctors lost registration, damaging the doctor–patient relationship.
- **Universities.** Institutions such as Monash became financially tied to Moderna; he cites Kylie Wagstaff's ivermectin research as delayed for years.

*Context:* Taken together, these describe a closed loop: if funders shape trials, journals publish them, regulators act on them, and media police dissent, then no internal mechanism remains to correct an error. That is the structural argument behind his call for an independent inquiry and advisory body.

## Part 4: Epilogue, postscript and addendum

- **Lessons:** Outcomes in airway infection depend on the biology of infection, not vaccine delivery; antigen dose matters; Australia must regain independence.
- **Alternative approach:** Promotes an oral "immunobiotic" of killed airway bacteria developed by his Newcastle group, said to strengthen the gut-to-airway pathway of mucosal protection. *(Context: this is the positive counterpart to his critique — a mucosal route to stimulate mucosal immunity — and the reader should note the author's personal stake in it.)*
- **Anecdote:** A post-vaccine-syndrome patient unable to get a prescription improved after taking ivermectin sheep drench. The summary's author explicitly advises against this.
- **Postscript:** Quotes a Gemini Deep Research analysis by Ronald Kostoff echoing his mRNA concerns.
- **Addendum:** Reproduces a rejected review article compiling his evidence on waning efficacy, tolerance, contamination, and adverse events including cancer, Alzheimer's and myocarditis.

## Points of dispute

Since these are claims rather than settled findings, it's worth knowing where mainstream evidence pushes back:

- **Early predictions:** The point that injected vaccines curb severe disease more than infection is broadly accepted; this part of his framework overlaps with mainstream immunology.
- **Ivermectin and HCQ:** Several large randomised trials, including outpatient and early-treatment trials (e.g. TOGETHER, ACTIV-6, PRINCIPLE for ivermectin; multiple outpatient HCQ trials), found no meaningful benefit. Clancy disputes their design, but the "only hospital patients were studied" argument does not cover all of them.
- **IgG4 and tolerance:** IgG4 class switching after repeated mRNA doses is documented; whether it causes clinically meaningful tolerance or more infection is unproven.
- **12% post-vaccine syndrome:** Drawn from a survey; self-selected survey samples generally can't establish population prevalence or causation.
- **VAERS / TGA death reports:** Passive reporting systems record reports, not confirmed causes, and Covid vaccines were given at unprecedented scale under heightened attention.
- **Excess deaths:** Attribution of non-Covid excess deaths is genuinely debated, with competing explanations including delayed care, undiagnosed Covid, and post-infection cardiovascular effects; dose-to-death correlations do not establish causation.
- **Veterinary ivermectin:** Animal formulations are dosed for livestock and have caused human poisonings; they should not be used.
