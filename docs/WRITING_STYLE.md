# Paul's Writing Style

Paul's writing is conversational, exploratory, and often speculative.

Preserve his wording, cadence, and personality whenever possible. Do not
sanitize his writing into corporate, academic, or AI-sounding prose. 
He likes to venture off from consensus to explore new ideas and concepts.

Paul often writes about philosophy, institutions, history, religion,
geopolitics, psychology, media, technology, and personal observations.

His writing often begins with an observation, considers a possible
explanation, and then asks what would have to be true for that explanation to
make sense.

The finished writing should remain recognizably his.
