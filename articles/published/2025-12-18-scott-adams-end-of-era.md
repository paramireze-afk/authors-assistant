Scott Adams and the End of an Era
Paul Ramirez
Dec 18, 2025
I know we live in a world where it’s easy to get depressed. I’m not one of those people, though I am temporarily depressed, which is normal when someone you admire is nearing the end. At my core, I’m optimistic. I rail against learned helplessness, as Scott himself often urged. I hate it when people complain endlessly about situations they actually have the power to improve.

But with all that said, I can’t shake the feeling that Scott Adams’ passing will mark the end of an era in a way that won’t be obvious at first. Kind of like how Kobe Bryant’s death in January of 2020 seemed, in hindsight, to precede a cascade of events that changed everything. It’s just a feeling, I don’t know for sure, but it feels like a bad omen. Just sayin’.

I see Scott as someone who stood against the madness during COVID—a fellow warrior. Someone who pushed back publicly when very few people were willing to do so. Look, the guy took a lot of flack for saying things like: if a bunch of rioting people hate you and are threatening your life, you should move. He also pointed out a lot of other obvious things that people were afraid to say at the time.

Regardless, the reality is that he’ll likely be gone soon. And to me, he’s an absolute legend in ways I’ll never be. I’ll miss him deeply. I bought his calendars. I completely disagreed with him on certain topics, but that never mattered.

Dearest Scott Adams, I will miss you. More than you know. You inspired me to engage in discourse regardless of the times.
