Andrew Kolvet: Reference Document
Prepared for investigative research purposes. Distinguishes documented facts, public statements, allegations, and unresolved questions throughout.

Who He Is
Andrew Kolvet is the primary public spokesperson for Turning Point USA (TPUSA) and, since Charlie Kirk’s assassination on September 10, 2025, one of the most consequential figures in determining what TPUSA becomes next. He is not a household name in conservative media, but he operates at the center of nearly every significant decision, communication, and controversy surrounding Kirk’s death and its aftermath.

He is described consistently by those who knew the organization as Charlie Kirk’s “constant companion” and right-hand man [8]. His proximity to Kirk made him one of the last people in regular contact with Kirk before the assassination, and one of the first insiders to respond to it.

Professional Background
Kolvet’s pre-TPUSA career includes stints at Fox News and the Tucker Carlson show, from both of which he was reportedly terminated [2]. The circumstances of those departures are not publicly documented in detail, but investigators note them as relevant context when evaluating his credibility and his later public dismissal of Tucker Carlson-adjacent skepticism about the official narrative.

His entry into TPUSA is itself a subject of scrutiny. According to Kolvet’s own account (given in a New York Times podcast interview), he was directed toward Charlie Kirk by Johnnie Moore, who spotted Kirk at a Southern Florida event and encouraged Kolvet to make contact [17]. At the time, TPUSA was primarily an economic liberty organization with no significant religious or foreign policy component. Investigators have noted that this origin story mirrors the pattern by which Kirk’s earlier mentor, Bill Montgomery, entered his orbit, raising questions about whether Kolvet’s introduction was as casual as presented [17].

Johnnie Moore connection: Moore is a prominent figure in pro-Israel evangelical circles and a key node in the network that, according to independent investigators, progressively redirected TPUSA toward pro-Israel foreign policy advocacy. Kolvet’s recruitment into Kirk’s orbit, facilitated by Moore, is considered significant context.

Role Within TPUSA
Kolvet’s formal title is spokesperson, but his functional role was considerably broader. He:

Managed Charlie Kirk’s public communications and media bookings
Served as the primary interface between Kirk and major donors
Had visibility into internal financial and organizational decisions
Controlled significant aspects of Kirk’s daily schedule
Was Kirk’s primary text and call contact for professional matters
This combination of roles gave Kolvet unusual leverage: he shaped what Kirk said publicly, who Kirk met with, and when. Investigators have noted that this level of access, in the context of a disputed assassination, makes Kolvet’s actions in the days before and after September 10 particularly worth examining [8].

Relationship with Charlie Kirk
By all accounts, Kolvet and Kirk were professionally inseparable. Kolvet was texting Kirk about talking points for the Utah event (specifically about polygamy and monogamy arguments) as late as approximately 11:50 AM on September 10, roughly 33 minutes before Kirk was shot [13].

The relationship was not without tension. Kolvet booked a Ben Shapiro interview for Kirk on September 9, the day before the assassination, that Kirk “was not super pleased” about [12]. Kolvet acknowledged this but said there was “a good reason for doing it.” This scheduling decision is notable because Kolvet was simultaneously aware of Kirk’s text messages expressing fear for his life and his desire to distance himself from the pro-Israel donor network.

Kirk had sent Kolvet messages expressing that he had “no choice but to leave the pro-Israel cause” and, separately, that “they are going to kill me” [1]. Kolvet received these messages, turned them over to federal authorities, and then publicly dismissed conspiracy theories about Kirk’s death. That contradiction sits at the center of why investigators keep returning to him.

The Pre-Death Text Messages: What Kolvet Knew
This is one of the most documented and contested areas involving Kolvet.

What is documented:

Kirk sent messages to Kolvet via Signal and/or Telegram expressing his intention to leave the pro-Israel cause [5]
Kirk sent Kolvet a message saying “they are going to kill me” the night before his death [1]
Kolvet turned these messages over to federal authorities, indicating he believed they might be relevant to an international conspiracy [2]
Kolvet confirmed the text messages Candace Owens released publicly were authentic [21]
What is disputed:

Erika Kirk initially denied any such messages existed, claiming to have checked Charlie’s iMessage. TPUSA later acknowledged the messages existed on Signal and Telegram, which Erika had not checked [5]. Whether this was an honest oversight or deliberate denial is unresolved.
Kolvet reportedly asked for the “they’re going to kill me” information to be kept off the record. When Erika implied the claim was false, the source made it public [1].
The core contradiction: Kolvet privately suspected an international conspiracy (his own stated reason for turning over the texts), while publicly appearing on the Charlie Kirk Show expressing bewilderment at why anyone would question the lone-gunman narrative [2]. These two positions cannot both be sincere.

September 10: Kolvet’s Movements and Timeline Anomalies
Kolvet’s whereabouts and actions on the day of the assassination contain several unresolved inconsistencies.

What Kolvet has said:

He was sitting at his desk when he received a call from a team member saying “they shot Charlie” [13]
He learned Kirk had died approximately 30 to 40 minutes after the shooting, placing this between 12:53 PM and 1:03 PM Utah time [9]
He “flew straight to Provo” after learning of the death [9]
What flight records suggest:

Plane N560TW departed Provo for Santa Barbara nearly two hours before Kirk was shot, suggesting someone anticipated Kolvet would need transport before the event occurred [10]
If Kolvet boarded a 12:33 PM Santa Barbara flight, he could not simultaneously have been sitting at his desk when he received the call at approximately 12:28 PM [10]
A second plane, N102DZ, repositioned from Salt Lake City to Scottsdale after flying Kirk to Utah, then flew a Scottsdale-to-Provo leg later that afternoon, likely associated with Kolvet’s arrival at the hospital [24]
The unexplained absence: Kolvet, described as Kirk’s constant companion, has not explained why he was not present at the Utah Valley University event on September 10 [8]. No public account addresses this directly.

The timeline contradiction: Kolvet told the New York Times he knew Kirk had died 30 to 40 minutes after the shooting. He separately told Candace Owens that Kirk still had a pulse at 1:19 PM Utah time [10]. These statements conflict with each other and with TPUSA’s public messaging that Kirk was “stable.”

The “Magic Bullet” Statement
On September 21, 2025, Kolvet posted on X claiming to quote Kirk’s surgeon directly [23]. The post stated the surgeon said “absolutely it should have gone through” and described Kirk’s bone density as so impressive that “he’s like the man of steel,” suggesting the bullet should have killed those standing behind Kirk.

TPUSA subsequently acknowledged that while Kolvet did speak with the surgeon, the surgeon did not know Kolvet would post the tweet and did not authorize it [18]. Dr. Lee Troder later denied making these statements, and sharing such detailed patient information publicly would constitute a potential HIPAA violation [23].

Independent investigators have characterized this as Kolvet fabricating or significantly embellishing a medical account to support a narrative about the shooting’s physical mechanics, at a time when the .30-06 ballistics were already being questioned [18].

The Warning to Candace Owens and Tucker Carlson
Within 72 hours of Kirk’s assassination, Kolvet called Candace Owens and told her “it was supposed to be you.” He warned that she and Tucker Carlson were on a list and needed to lay low. He did not reveal the source of this information [6].

A separate individual, described as a woman married to a CIA contractor, delivered the same warning about Owens and Carlson being targeted [6].

Kolvet has not publicly addressed this warning or explained its source. The fact that he issued it privately while publicly promoting the lone-gunman narrative is one of the most frequently cited contradictions in the independent investigation.

Kolvet’s Post-Assassination Role
After Kirk’s death, Kolvet became one of the primary voices of TPUSA’s public-facing operation. The Charlie Kirk Show continued broadcasting with hosts including Jack Posobiec and Kolvet himself [15].

Investigators have raised the theory that Kolvet is positioning himself as the natural successor to lead TPUSA, and that his management of Erika Kirk’s public appearances (including styling, speech coaching, and interview timing) may be deliberately undermining her in order to clear that path [16]. This remains an allegation without documentary support, but it is noted as a recurring analytical thread.

The DOGE audit: Eight days before his death, Kirk wrote an internal memo promoting Justin Streiff as COO, removing Tyler Bowyer from that role, and announcing a new DOGE-style financial audit separate from the standard annual audit [22]. After Kirk’s death, Tyler Bowyer publicly stated no audit was necessary. The DOGE audit was cancelled. Kolvet’s role in this sequence, if any, is not documented.

Kolvet and the Johnnie Moore / Pro-Israel Network
The most recent investigative thread (as of May 2026) concerns Kolvet’s relationship with Johnnie Moore and the broader pro-Israel evangelical donor network that Moore represents [17].

The theory, developed by Baron Coleman, is that Kolvet was introduced to Kirk not casually but as part of a deliberate effort to redirect TPUSA toward pro-Israel foreign policy advocacy. Kirk’s leaked text messages about losing a $2 million Jewish donor and considering leaving the pro-Israel cause are interpreted, within this framework, as a death sentence within the structure Kolvet and Moore had built around him [16].

After Kirk’s death, TPUSA moved decisively in the pro-Israel direction Kirk had been resisting. Money flowed in. The concerns about Tucker Carlson and Candace Owens speaking at events were resolved by their exclusion. The DOGE audit was cancelled [7].

Whether Kolvet was an architect of this outcome, an instrument of it, or a bystander who happened to be positioned at the center of it is one of the central unresolved questions of the investigation.

Kolvet’s Credibility: A Summary of Documented Problems
For any researcher, the following documented credibility issues are worth noting before treating Kolvet’s public statements as reliable:

Fabricated or unauthorized surgeon quote about Kirk’s bullet wound [18]
Contradictory accounts of when he learned Kirk had died [9]
Contradictory public vs. private positions on whether Kirk’s death involved an international conspiracy [2]
Unexplained absence from Kirk’s side on the day of the assassination [8]
Flight timeline that may be inconsistent with his stated location when he received news of the shooting [10]
Issued private warnings about a hit list while publicly dismissing conspiracy theories [6]
Scheduled an interview Kirk did not want, the day before Kirk’s death, despite knowing Kirk feared for his life [12]
None of these individually constitute proof of wrongdoing. Collectively, they represent a pattern that investigators have found difficult to explain through innocent coincidence.

Why Andrew Kolvet Matters
Kolvet matters because he sits at the intersection of every major unresolved question in this case.

He was the last person in regular professional contact with Kirk before the assassination. He received Kirk’s explicit fear-of-death messages and chose to turn them over to federal authorities, an action that implicitly acknowledged the possibility of a conspiracy, while simultaneously dismissing that possibility in public. He was absent from the event where Kirk was killed. His stated timeline of events on September 10 contains documented inconsistencies. He fabricated or embellished a medical account that shaped early public understanding of the shooting’s physical mechanics. He warned two of the investigation’s most prominent voices that they were on a hit list, then never explained where that intelligence came from.

Beyond the specific anomalies, Kolvet represents a structural question: in an organization where the founder was just assassinated, who actually controls TPUSA now? The answer, by most accounts, runs through Kolvet. That makes him not just a witness or a suspect in the narrow sense, but a key to understanding what TPUSA is, who it serves, and what it is being used for.

Any serious investigation of Charlie Kirk’s death that does not account for Andrew Kolvet’s role, his contradictions, and his network connections is incomplete.
