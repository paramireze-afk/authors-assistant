# Turning Point USA (TPUSA)

## Quick Reference

- **Type:** 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization
- **Founded:** 2012
- **Founder:** Charlie Kirk (age 18 at founding)
- **Co-founder:** Bill Montgomery (died July 2020, COVID, age 80)
- **Original mission:** Economic libertarianism, free markets, limited government, fiscal responsibility (explicitly secular)
- **Current mission (post-2019):** Judeo-Christian nationalism, pro-Israel engagement, conservative youth organizing
- **Headquarters:** Scottsdale, Arizona
- **Annual revenue (peak):** ~$85-95 million (FY2024)
- **Employees (peak):** 700+
- **Current CEO:** Erika Kirk (appointed September 18, 2025, 8 days after Charlie Kirk's assassination)

## Founding and Early Years (2012-2018)

Charlie Kirk was discovered at age 18 by Bill Montgomery while speaking at Benedictine University during a mock demonstration comparing the Tea Party and Occupy Wall Street movements. Montgomery was so impressed he told Kirk to skip college and start an organization instead. For the first two and a half years, Montgomery traveled with Kirk because Kirk was too young to rent cars or book hotel rooms. [Source: Baron Coleman, Episode 11]

The organization's name is widely believed to have been chosen to leverage the "TP" initials associated with the Tea Party movement, which would have been too toxic a brand for reaching college students directly.

Kirk took no salary for the first five years (2013-2018). Revenue grew slowly:
- 2013: ~$78,000
- 2018: ~$11 million

Through this period, TPUSA was avowedly secular. Archive captures of the website from March 2019 and July 2021 show zero references to faith, Christianity, God, or Jesus. The mission statement was essentially a Von Mises Institute for young people.

Officers through 2018: Bill Montgomery, Charlie Kirk, George Hamstra (only three listed).

## The Transformation (2018-2021)

### The Erika Frantzve Factor

In summer 2018, Charlie Kirk met Erika Frantzve through a TPUSA interview. Their relationship coincided with a dramatic organizational transformation:

- FY2018 revenue: ~$11 million
- FY2019 revenue: ~$29 million (nearly tripled in one year)
- Kirk's salary: jumped from ~$80,000 to ~$290,000-$300,000
- Seven new key employees added to leadership in the same year

Kirk and Erika married in a wedding funded by TPUSA. [Source: Baron Coleman, Episode 51; James Li video]

### The Rob McCoy Factor

After Bill Montgomery's death in July 2020, Kirk came under the influence of Ventura County pastor Rob McCoy. McCoy encouraged Kirk to fuse Christianity with conservative politics. Kirk credited McCoy with transforming TPUSA from a secular organization into one declaring God was on the side of conservatives.

TPUSA Faith launched during this period. The organization that had preached economic liberty pivoted to Judeo-Christian nationalism.

### The Israel Pivot

Alongside the religious transformation came a decisive pro-Israel orientation. Major Zionist donors, including Bob Shillman and others connected to the David Horowitz Freedom Center, had funded TPUSA from early on. The post-2018 transformation deepened this relationship. By 2024, TPUSA was explicitly pro-Israel and pro-Zionist in its programming and donor base.

### Financial Red Flags (pre-assassination)
- FY2024: TPUSA overspent by ~$10 million, depleting $23 million in net assets
- May 15, 2025: Only ONE of four required Form 990 filings submitted. Forms for Turning Point Endowment, Turning Point Action, and America's Turning Point were never filed. [Source 9]
- $8.6 million sent to "America's Turning Point" (a shell company with zero reported employees) with no public accounting
- October 15, 2025: TPUSA missed the tax extension deadline as well. Baker Tilly issued a "clean audit" without flagging the missing forms. [Source 11]
- September 2, 2025: Kirk requested a DOGE-style independent audit (separate from the annual audit) and removed Tyler Bowyer as COO. After Kirk's death, Bowyer publicly stated no additional audit was necessary. The DOGE audit was cancelled. [Source 10]

### Post-Assassination Finances
- TPUSA raised approximately $90 million within two weeks of Kirk's death
- Merchandise was sold at Kirk's memorial
- A quarter-billion dollar fundraising figure has been cited for the post-assassination period
- $150 million from Benjamin Netanyahu (or connected donors) reportedly accepted after Kirk's death, money Kirk had explicitly rejected while alive [Source: Brandi and Billie, Episode 5 whistleblower interview]

## Governance Structure

TPUSA is a nonprofit controlled by a board of directors. Charlie Kirk did not "own" the organization. The board controls all decisions, money, and employment.

**Current board (as of late 2025):**
- Erika Kirk (CEO and Chairwoman)
- David Engelhardt
- Doug DeGroote (also Kirk's personal financial advisor)
- Mike Miller
- Tom Sedaika

The four non-Erika board members could vote Erika out at any time. Erika serves at the board's pleasure. This structure is legally required in all 50 states for nonprofits to prevent single-person control of tax-free money.

Doug DeGroote's dual role (personal financial advisor to Kirk AND TPUSA board member) is a notable conflict of interest worth tracking.

## TPUSA as Controlled Opposition (Theory)

Baron Coleman and others have argued that TPUSA functioned as a psychological operation to keep conservative activists within controlled ideological boundaries, similar to how the Q movement kept Trump supporters passive. Under this theory:

- Kirk was a manufactured product selected and funded by wealthy donors to manage a specific political space
- TPUSA's role was to police acceptable discourse on the right (Kirk's texts to Dinesh D'Souza attacking Nick Fuentes as "vermin" are cited as an example of this enforcement mechanism)
- Kirk was killed because he was taking the "widget factory" (the infrastructure, methods, and donor relationships built with hundreds of millions in investment) to work for competitors or in a direction donors could not control
- The Christian fundamentalist branding was not organic but was introduced later as a donor-driven reorientation

## The Israel Relationship

This is central to understanding TPUSA's transformation and the motive theories around Kirk's assassination.

- TPUSA's early funding came substantially from Zionist donors including the David Horowitz Freedom Center
- The post-2018 transformation deepened pro-Israel orientation
- Kirk repaid donors with anti-Palestinian content, propaganda trips to Israel, and suppression of nationalist voices challenging Israel at TPUSA events
- By 2024, Kirk was under intense pressure from pro-Israel donors as his base shifted against Israel post-October 7
- Kirk privately began questioning Israeli influence, the Epstein-Israel connection, and whether Israel allowed October 7 to proceed
- His July 2025 Student Action Summit featured Tucker Carlson, Megyn Kelly, and Dave Smith openly criticizing Israel and Zionist donors
- Following the summit, Kirk received dozens of threatening texts from pro-Israel donors and described being "frightened" [Source 5]
- Approximately 48 hours before his assassination, Kirk told TPUSA staff, donors, and a rabbi that he was leaving the pro-Israel cause [Source 7]
- Netanyahu called Kirk, offered to take TPUSA "to the next level" financially. Kirk declined. [Source 5] Source: Baron Coleman Episode 55
- After Kirk's death: TPUSA executed a complete policy reversal on Israel and Iran, accepted the $150 million Kirk had refused, and became explicitly pro-Israel engagement under Erika's leadership
