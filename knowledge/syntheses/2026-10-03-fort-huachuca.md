# The Military's AI Leaked Its Own Guest List

## An incredibly embarrassing story you will certainly never hear

First, some background. There's an ongoing controversy over whether Mitch Snow, a former special forces operator, was right when he said he saw key members of Charlie Kirk's security team, as well as Erika Kirk, at Fort Huachuca, the US's premier intelligence base, in the day or days before the assassination.

So why does Fort Huachuca matter? That's a weird group to have heading to the same Army base in the same handful of days.

And then there's the HADES jet itself, a new Army spy plane and the flagship program of the recon task force at Fort Huachuca. Why did it fly over the Provo area twice on the day of the assassination, and again during Tyler Robinson's arrest?

So how does anyone know who was expected on that base? This is where it gets crazy: Google Trends. Over the past few months, they keep finding hits for the same kinds of people: senior officials, searched by their exact titles and photos, around the base, on very specific days.

Which raises an obvious question. If Mitch Snow was lying, or just wrong, why was the military's own system running Google searches on the same people he claimed to see, in the same window of time?
