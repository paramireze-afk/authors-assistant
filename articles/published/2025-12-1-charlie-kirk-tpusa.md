Hi Everyone, I don’t normally post on Reddit but curious if there are people who think like I do. When it comes to the Charlie Kirk assasination, my hypothesis is that he may have been a casualty of a much larger political war. For context, hundreds of journalists and people in peacekeeping or activist roles have been killed since the start of the Gaza conflict, so the idea of political violence isn’t far-fetched. What fascinates me about the Charlie Kirk assassination is how little the “lone gunman” theory actually explains. Even the mommy sleuths on TikTok have pointed out that the Tyler Robinson text messages don’t match the official timeline — and gun people online have noted that the level of rifle disassembly and reassembly he supposedly did in minutes would make him some kind of world-champion armorer. Then there’s the “miracle neck muscles” thing: early reports claimed a .30-06 round was stopped by muscle tissue, which ballistic experts say is physically impossible. People have posted slow-motion tests showing even the weakest .30-06 load would pass clean through a moose neck, let alone a human one.

And the financial side gets even stranger. I could go on for a long time about the alleged fraud raised by channels like WolvesAndFinance, but the core issue is that something about TPUSA feels off. A lot of the people who operate around the organization appear to have military, naval, or intelligence-adjacent backgrounds, which is at least an interesting pattern. And there are videos circulating where pastors across multiple states seem to repeat almost identical talking points, which only adds to the oddness. Anyone else fascinated by this? The way I see it, some stories diagnose the entire system — much like the COVID story did — and this one feels like it might be in that category.
