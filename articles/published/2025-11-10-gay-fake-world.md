The Gay/Fake world and Jordan Peterson
A brief summary of my fucked up thoughts
Paul Ramirez
Nov 10, 2025
I’ve been having a lot of dreams lately, many about subjugation at a societal level. Is that weird already? But yeah, I honestly think about how everything today, as far as media or politics goes, just feels “fake and/or gay.” I mean that literally, not knocking anyone’s sexuality, but it seems true to me. I’m not religious or homophobic; it’s more that there’s this growing “tyranny of the minority” vibe going on.

I guess I’m just old-school. Let me have my slapstick humor, parody movies. Can we go back to producing shows like Monty Python and Blazing Saddles. Can we make more Douglas Adams movies? Can we please go back to making movies like that again? Yes, I still read Tom Sawyer. That doesn’t make me a bigot. And stop torturing me because I think J.K. Rowling is awesome, okay?

Make of that what you will, but it strikes me that a truly subjugated society is one that’s been cut off from its own past, its roots, and its traditions. A culture with no sense of its own past with cultural context. And honestly, I think I’ve been partially subjugated myself, because somewhere along the way I lost touch with Jordan Peterson, and his struggle with life-threatening illness.

https://x.com/MikhailaFuller/status/1974590350367875325

I used to be a hardcore atheist, the kind who actually thought someone like Sam Harris was intelligent. But Jordan broke me out of that mindset. He taught me about hypothetical truths and symbolism in meaning. He taught me that theres wisdom hidden in stories, biblical or otherwise, even Disney ones. He taught me to look past the veneer of the immediate surface of things, and everything changed for me then. That’s around when I started to drift from my usual friend groups, back in 2015 or so. You could say I accidentally adopted a kind of Darwinian worldview, if that makes sense.

Something shifted with Jordan around 2018, when his fame went global. Did anyone else notice that? His story got darker, the media turned on him viciously, and while his wife Tammy was fighting a rare, life-threatening cancer, he was under enormous pressure. The nonstop criticism and public attacks took a heavy toll on him and camera’s were all over him and his family.

Then he was in a seven-day coma in Russia (Russia???) for a benzo overdose. You know, the guy who writes books about discipline and self-control somehow landed in Russia for a seven day coma? By the way, for reference, around that same time, Kanye West posted a text from his trainer threatening him with forced hospitalization if he didn’t cooperate.


https://x.com/DndaTimes/status/1588309634720231424






Perhaps it’s all fake.. but… it matches a pattern with a lot of celebrities if one is inclined to go down such a rabbit hole. Because if a person stops to think about it, what is subjugation othen then control over messaging, over narratives. To make the public believe exactly as you wish it to do.

Anyway, I’ve realized how little attention I’ve paid to Jordan’s health lately, and that realization hits me hard. It feels like a symptom of my own partial subjugation, honestly. I wish I could be there for him now the way he was there for me when I was going through the darkest periods of my life. When nothing made sense but through discipline I was able to get my life together using his 12 rules for life.
