# Shaped Charge Theory: Research Reference

## What This Document Is

This is a research reference for investigators examining the shaped charge theory as it relates to the death of Charlie Kirk on September 10, 2025. It does not argue for or against the theory. It maps the claims, the evidence, the reasoning, the gaps, and the disagreements so that any investigator can orient themselves quickly and work from a shared baseline.

The theory is controversial. It is also, by the assessment of multiple independent analysts with relevant technical backgrounds, not easily dismissed. Both of those things are true simultaneously.

## Background: Why This Theory Exists

The official narrative holds that Charlie Kirk was shot in the neck by a .30-06 rifle fired by Tyler James Robinson from a rooftop approximately 100 yards away. Robinson was arrested roughly 33 hours later. He has not confessed.

The shaped charge theory emerged because several investigators found the official ballistics account physically difficult to reconcile with the video evidence and the reported wound characteristics. The theory proposes that Kirk was not killed by a distant rifle shot at all, but by a miniaturized explosive device concealed inside the wireless lapel microphone he was wearing at the time of the event.

The theory was developed primarily by Jon Bray, an analyst with a background in body armor design and military refurbishment programs. It has since been examined, tested, and debated across multiple independent investigation platforms, including Ian Carroll, Baron Coleman, and the Brandi and Billie Show.

## The Theory: A Plain-Language Summary

The core claim is this: a shaped charge, approximately one to two grams of high-density explosive, was concealed inside Kirk’s wireless lapel microphone. At the moment of detonation, the charge fired a high-velocity jet of energy into Kirk’s chest or neck, producing a wound consistent with a high-powered rifle impact while generating an outward pressure wave visible in the video footage as the shirt ballooning upward before any neck wound appears.

The device would have been undetectable to standard security screening. It would have functioned normally as a microphone until triggered. Detonation could have been achieved by remote signal, timer, or by programming the device’s onboard firmware to trigger upon detecting a specific acoustic signature, such as a supersonic crack.

The rifle shot, under this theory, either did not happen at all, or was a coordinated cover story designed to provide a plausible cause of death and a suspect to arrest.

## How the Theory Developed

### Stage 1: The Shirt Anomaly

Jon Bray’s initial entry point was not explosives. His background in body armor led him to suspect Kirk might have been wearing a vest, based on the shirt’s behavior in the video. That hypothesis was ruled out by close-up footage showing no vest.

What remained unexplained was the shirt movement itself. Using modified MIT Labs optical flow software to track pixel displacement frame by frame, Bray identified an epicenter of movement in Kirk’s upper sternum area. The shirt does not move inward, as it would if struck by an incoming projectile. It moves outward and upward, consistent with a pressure wave originating at the chest and pushing away from the body [15].

Three distinct energetic events were identified in the video:

- The shirt pulling upward toward Kirk’s face
- A magnetic clasp moving laterally across his neck
- A necklace flying over his head

All three precede the visible neck wound. The sequence matters: if a bullet caused the neck wound, the shirt movement should follow the wound, not precede it.

### Stage 2: The Shaped Charge Hypothesis

Once the outward pressure wave was established as the primary observation requiring explanation, Bray investigated whether a shaped charge could produce it. A shaped charge is a directional explosive that uses a conical geometry to focus detonation energy into a high-velocity jet rather than a radial blast. The jet travels at Mach 7 or above, turning contacted material to liquid and creating deep penetrating wounds with a very small entry profile [10].

Key technical findings from this stage:

- One to two grams of explosive is sufficient to replicate the terminal ballistics of a .30-06 round at 150 yards [9]
- A shaped charge of this size can fit inside a Rode wireless lapel microphone after replacing the standard 350 milliamp battery with a 250 milliamp battery and using the freed space for the charge and primer [9]
- The Rode wireless Pro model contains a millisecond-precision timing chip designed for camera synchronization, which could serve as a precision detonation trigger [9]
- Custom firmware could be uploaded via USB during battery replacement, programming the device to trigger on a specific acoustic frequency [9]
- From the outside, the modified device would be indistinguishable from a standard microphone [6]

### Stage 3: The Mounting Angle Problem and Trajectory Explanation

One complication the theory had to address: if the device was designed to fire a shaped charge jet into Kirk’s chest, why was the wound in his neck?

Bray’s explanation involves the microphone’s mounting orientation. The device was clipped on its side rather than flat against the chest. When the primer charge fired to create standoff distance, the sideways mounting caused the charge to lift upward rather than fire directly inward, redirecting the jet’s trajectory across Kirk’s chest and toward his neck [10].

This explanation accounts for the reported burn marks under Kirk’s right nipple, which would be consistent with the primer charge rather than the shaped charge itself [12].

### Stage 4: The AES Contract

The theory gained significant additional weight when Baron Coleman identified a Department of Defense contract awarded to Accurate Energetic Systems (AES) of McEwen, Tennessee [22].

Contract details:

- Contract number: N0016425PJ538
- Description: “MINIATURIZED-XS DEMOLITION CHARGES AND DEMOLITION CHARGES, ANTI PERSONNEL-XS TO SUPPORT SPM”
- Award date: May 1, 2025
- Delivery date: August 25, 2025
- Value: $440,494
- This is the only contract in the USAspending.gov database since 2008 using the terms “MINIATURIZED-XS” or “ANTI PERSONNEL-XS” [tl_250, tl_252]

The delivery date of August 25 is sixteen days before Kirk’s death. It also coincides with the date Brian Harpole, Kirk’s head of security, stated that security planning for the UVU event began [1].

AES’s documented capabilities include shaped charge fabrication, claymore mine manufacturing, precision explosive pressing, and load/assemble/pack operations. These are precisely the capabilities required to manufacture a miniaturized explosively formed penetrator small enough to fit inside a consumer microphone [1].

### Stage 5: The AES Explosion

On October 10, 2025, the AES production facility in Dickson, Tennessee was catastrophically destroyed in an explosion that killed 16 employees and leveled the building to its foundation [tl_252, Source 2, Source 3, Source 4].

This was the deadliest U.S. industrial accident in 34 years. AES had operated safely for 43 years before this event.

The explosion occurred two days after Jon Bray publicly elaborated the exploding microphone theory in detail (October 7 to 8, 2025).

Additional details that investigators have flagged:

- The production manager at the time of the explosion had only been in the role for 30 days
- The previous production manager left the position shortly after the September 10 UVU event
- The previous production manager cannot be located through any available contact information [3]
- The explosion received minimal national news coverage despite being the deadliest U.S. industrial accident in decades [8]
- Two AES employees who were contacted stated they could not comment due to an ongoing investigation [8]

## The Pager Attack Parallel

Investigators have drawn a direct comparison to the September 2024 Israeli pager attacks on Hezbollah, in which communication devices containing small explosive charges were detonated simultaneously across Lebanon. Those devices:

- Functioned normally as pagers until detonation
- Passed through airport security screening without detection (Hamas reportedly ran pagers through scanners specifically looking for explosives and found nothing) [6]
- Produced sounds described as pops or cracks rather than conventional explosions
- Were detonated remotely by signal

Multiple UVU witnesses described the sound at the Kirk event as a “pop” or “firecrackers,” not a rifle crack. The suppressed eyewitness, a firearms instructor with timestamped footage from 12:22 PM, specifically described the sound as a “sharp crack instead of a big hunting rifle’s boom” and assessed the ammunition as consistent with a lightweight .223 round rather than a .30-06 [7].

The pager attack comparison is relevant not only technically but in terms of attribution. The pager attacks are widely attributed to Israeli intelligence. The shaped charge theory, combined with the foreknowledge data showing searches of Kirk’s venue and security personnel from Israeli IP addresses months before the shooting [tl_346, tl_344], has led investigators to examine Israeli state involvement as a serious hypothesis.

## The Crime Scene Paving

Four days after the assassination, on a Sunday, the grass where Kirk was killed was paved over [21].

Cyclonite (RDX), a common explosive used in shaped charges, does not absorb into soil. It persists as a detectable residue. If a shaped charge detonated at the event site, explosive residue would remain in the soil and on surrounding surfaces, potentially indefinitely.

Investigators have noted that paving the site would be the most effective method of eliminating this residue from forensic access. The paving was not in UVU’s capital improvement budget. The Division of Facilities Construction Management stated in writing that no contracts or bids exist for the project. The paperwork shows same-day retail receipts from Lowe’s and a contractor invoice issued after the work was completed [21].

## Physical Fragments

Investigators have identified what appear to be black specks visible in the air at the moment of impact in the video footage. A fragment is visible striking the tent roof above Kirk. Shattered fragments were reportedly found on the floor of the SUV that transported Kirk from the scene. These fragments are described as consistent with ABS plastic, the same material used in Rode wireless microphone housings [15].

Kirk’s left hand shows what appear to be cuts rather than blood splatter in casket video footage, which investigators suggest is consistent with secondary shrapnel from an exploded device rather than a gunshot wound.

These observations are based on video analysis by independent investigators. No independent forensic examination of the fragments has been publicly confirmed.

## Sound Characteristics

Shaped charges produce a distinctive sound profile that differs from conventional explosives and firearms:

- Energy is directed in a concentrated beam rather than radiating outward
- People positioned in front of the charge (opposite the jet direction) would hear very little
- People positioned to the side, where escaping gas vents, would hear a sound described as a firework or sharp crack [13]
- The sound does not produce the deep boom characteristic of a .30-06 rifle

This is consistent with witness descriptions of the sound at UVU and with the suppressed eyewitness’s assessment that the sound was inconsistent with a hunting rifle.

## Technical Accessibility

Jon Bray and Ian Carroll have both addressed the question of whether this technology is exotic or widely accessible [5]:

- Shaped charge cones can be fabricated with a 3D printer using copper filament
- The most difficult component is high-density explosive, but small quantities are accessible through oil and gas industry connections, demolition work, and other industrial applications
- Nation states worldwide possess this capability, but it is not exclusively a state-level technology
- Multiple individuals with military explosives experience, including Marine Corps personnel, assessed the theory as 92 to 94 percent plausible based on their knowledge of shaped charges [14]

The AES contract, however, suggests state-level procurement rather than improvised manufacture.

## Disagreements Among Investigators

### On the wound location

The theory was originally designed to explain a chest wound. The actual wound was in the neck. Bray’s mounting-angle explanation (the sideways clip redirecting the jet upward) addresses this, but some investigators find this explanation less satisfying than the core theory. The question of whether the device could reliably produce a neck wound from a chest-mounted microphone remains a point of discussion.

### On the “why use traceable procurement” problem

If a state actor used a government procurement contract to manufacture the assassination device, that contract is publicly searchable. Some investigators find this implausible as operational security. Others argue that the contract description is sufficiently obscure that it would not attract attention without the specific context of the Kirk investigation, and that the AES explosion was intended to eliminate the physical evidence trail even if the paper trail survived.

### On whether a rifle shot also occurred

Some investigators hold that the shaped charge theory and the rifle shot are mutually exclusive. Others propose a hybrid scenario in which a rifle was fired (accounting for the suppressed eyewitness’s observations) but the killing wound was delivered by the device, with the rifle shot serving as acoustic cover or as a backup mechanism. This remains unresolved.

### On the shirt movement interpretation

Ian Carroll initially expressed skepticism about the shirt movement as evidence, noting that the speed of the lift was the key observation demanding explanation [18]. His position evolved after learning more about shaped charge mechanics. Not all investigators who find the AES contract compelling also find the video analysis independently convincing, and vice versa.

## Core Claims of the Theory

- Charlie Kirk was killed by a miniaturized shaped charge concealed inside his wireless lapel microphone, not by a rifle shot from a distant shooter.
- The device fired a high-velocity jet of energy into Kirk’s body, producing a wound consistent with a .30-06 rifle impact.
- The outward pressure wave from the detonation is visible in video footage as the shirt ballooning upward before the neck wound appears, and this sequence is inconsistent with an incoming projectile.
- The device was undetectable by standard security screening and functioned normally as a microphone until detonation.
- A Department of Defense contract for miniaturized anti-personnel demolition charges was delivered to AES sixteen days before the shooting.
- The AES facility was destroyed two days after the theory was publicly elaborated, killing 16 people and eliminating physical evidence.
- The crime scene was paved over four days after the shooting, eliminating potential explosive residue evidence.
- The technology is consistent with Israeli pager attack methodology and with the broader foreknowledge pattern pointing toward Israeli state involvement.

## Criticisms and Alternative Explanations

- The wound location problem. The device was chest-mounted. The wound was in the neck. The mounting-angle explanation is plausible but has not been independently tested or verified. A device designed to produce a chest wound that instead produced a neck wound raises questions about reliability and intent.
- Traceable procurement. Using a publicly searchable government contract to procure an assassination weapon is an unusual operational security choice. Critics argue that a state actor capable of this operation would use covert procurement channels.
- The rifle evidence. The prosecution has presented a rifle, casings, and DNA evidence. If no rifle shot occurred, all of this evidence is fabricated. That is a significant claim requiring a significant evidence threshold. The shaped charge theory does not fully account for the physical evidence chain around the rifle, though investigators have raised serious questions about that chain independently.
- No independent forensic confirmation. The video analysis, fragment identification, and wound characteristic assessments are all from independent investigators without access to the physical evidence. No independent forensic laboratory has examined the microphone, the fragments, or the wound.
- The “why Kirk” problem. If a state actor deployed this level of sophistication, the choice of a lapel microphone as the delivery mechanism requires that actor to have had access to Kirk’s specific microphone before the event. This implies either advance access to his equipment or a supply chain compromise. Neither has been independently documented.
- The official narrative’s alternative. The prosecution’s account, if taken at face value, does explain the death without requiring any of this. The shaped charge theory is only necessary if the official ballistics account is false. Investigators who find the .30-06 wound characteristics implausible but not impossible may not find the shaped charge theory necessary.

## What Is Well Supported

- Kirk’s shirt moves outward before the neck wound appears. This is observable in the video and has been analyzed by multiple independent investigators using different methods.
- A DoD contract for miniaturized anti-personnel demolition charges was delivered to AES sixteen days before the shooting. This is a public record.
- The AES facility was destroyed thirty days after the shooting, killing 16 people. This is a public record.
- The explosion occurred two days after the theory was publicly elaborated. This is a documented timeline fact.
- Shaped charge technology is capable of producing the wound characteristics described in this case. This assessment comes from multiple individuals with relevant military and technical backgrounds.
- The pager attack precedent establishes that miniaturized explosive devices concealed in consumer electronics have been deployed in real-world assassination operations.
- The crime scene was paved over four days after the shooting with no standard procurement documentation.

## What Remains Uncertain

- Whether the shirt movement is definitively caused by an explosive device or could have an alternative explanation
- Whether the AES contract was specifically for the device used in this case, or for an unrelated application
- Whether the AES explosion was deliberate destruction of evidence or an industrial accident
- Whether the previous production manager’s disappearance is connected to the case
- How the device was introduced into Kirk’s microphone supply chain
- Whether a rifle shot also occurred alongside the device detonation
- Whether the neck wound trajectory is consistent with the mounting-angle explanation
- Whether any physical fragments have been independently examined
- Who specifically would have had access to Kirk’s microphone before the event
