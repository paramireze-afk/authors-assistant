# Pinocchio State

*Scope note: This concept note extracts Michael Yon's "Pinocchio" state metaphor from a single transcript-derived source — the previously documented interview referred to in this knowledge base as the "Suez, 'Pinocchio,' and Grand Strategy Reference" (dated by content to April 19, 2026). It documents his usage as presented in that one source and does not treat the concept as an established scholarly category, does not search for or incorporate additional material from elsewhere in his body of work, and does not extend, validate, or complete the framework beyond what the source itself contains.*

---

## The Metaphor as Yon Uses It

Yon borrows the term "Pinocchio" directly from the children's story: a wooden puppet, carved to be controlled by strings, that comes to life. In the version of the story Yon recounts, the puppet's first act upon coming to life is to attack its own creator.

He applies this as a metaphor for a state or political entity that is:

1. **Deliberately created** by an external, more powerful sponsor — in the one case elaborated in the source, he describes British intelligence "using Zionists" to establish Israel, motivated by a strategic need to secure control of the Suez Canal.
2. **Instrumentalized** — the created entity functions as a tool serving its creator's strategic interests, in this case control of a critical maritime chokepoint and surrounding resource-rich territory.
3. **Capable of developing beyond the creator's original intent** — Yon describes this using the phrase "the puppet grows heavier and heavier on the strings," suggesting a created entity can accumulate independent weight, capability, or agency over time.

Yon states explicitly regarding this framework: "This is not conspiracy theory. It's as common in war as bows and arrows and rifles" — presenting the instrumentalization of a political movement to secure a strategic asset as, in his view, an unremarkable and recurring feature of statecraft rather than something exceptional.

*Direct statement vs. inference: the definition above (points 1–3) is drawn directly from Yon's own wording in the source. The numbered structure is an organizational device applied to present his statements clearly; Yon does not present these as an explicit numbered list himself.*

## The Lifecycle Yon Describes

The source presents a four-stage progression, though Yon does not label these stages explicitly — the following structure is inferred from the sequence of his statements to organize them, not asserted by him as a formal model.

### 1. Creation
An external power (in the elaborated example, British intelligence) establishes or backs a political movement/entity to serve a specific strategic purpose. Yon states Israel was "organic but... also created" — holding both descriptions simultaneously rather than treating them as contradictory. He states the entity was later "nuclearized by British intelligence and then US took over later," describing a transfer of sponsorship over time.

### 2. Instrumental Use
The created entity serves its intended strategic function for its sponsor. In the Suez example, this function is control of the canal and the surrounding resource-rich region. Yon does not, in this source, describe this stage in further detail beyond stating the entity's original purpose.

### 3. Growth Beyond Original Control
Yon describes a puppet that can "grow" past the point of simple control, using the phrase "the strings go both ways" — suggesting a bidirectional relationship can develop between creator and creation. He offers two distinct possible outcomes at this stage, presented as alternatives rather than a single fixed path:
- The puppet turns on its creator (the literal Pinocchio story, as Yon recounts it, opens with the puppet attacking its maker).
- The puppet grows powerful enough that "it just pulls the string master down and now it's the master" — i.e., the created entity can, in some cases, come to effectively control or dominate its original sponsor.

### 4. Dismantling ("Overstaying Its Welcome")
Yon connects this framework to a direct, explicit prediction: he states he told a named associate (described as a former IDF special forces contact) that he believes **Israel will be destroyed**, framing this through the logic that a "Pinocchio" can "overstay its welcome" and be deliberately dismantled by the same category of actor that created it once it is no longer useful, or is judged to have grown too independent or too costly to maintain.

*Direct statement vs. inference: stages 1, 3, and the prediction of dismantling in stage 4 are drawn from direct statements in the source. Stage 2 (instrumental use) is thinly elaborated in the source itself — the source does not describe in detail how or for how long this stage is understood to operate before growth/dismantling begins. This gap is noted below.*

## Where the Source Does Not Provide Evidence or Elaboration

The following are explicitly flagged as gaps in the source material, not filled in here:

- **No named individuals, documents, or organizations** are cited as evidence that British intelligence specifically created the Zionist movement for this purpose. The source's evidentiary basis consists of a physical artifact Yon owns (a Jewish Colonial Trust share warrant bearing Theodor Herzl's name) and the independently documented historical fact of the Suez Canal's strategic importance to Britain — the causal claim connecting these (that intelligence services engineered the movement specifically for canal control) is Yon's own interpretive framework layered on top of that evidence, not something the source grounds in a specific named source.
- **No defined trigger or mechanism** is given for how or when a "Pinocchio" entity transitions from Stage 2 (instrumental use) to Stage 3 (growth beyond control) or Stage 4 (dismantling). The source does not specify what would cause a sponsor to judge an entity as having "overstayed its welcome," beyond the general claim that this judgment has been reached regarding Israel.
- **No timeframe** is given for the predicted dismantling of Israel described in Stage 4.
- **When asked directly to define "Zionist"** — the actor responsible for both creating and (predicted) destroying the state in this framework — Yon states the term resists precise definition, comparing the difficulty to defining "Arab" or "Catholic." The source therefore does not provide a working definition of the actor central to the framework, a gap Yon acknowledges himself rather than one identified externally.

## Historical Examples Yon Invokes (Within This Source)

- **The Suez Canal (opened 1869)** — described by Yon as "the spinal cord of the British Empire," and the specific strategic asset he states motivated the creation of Israel as a Pinocchio entity.
- **The Jewish Colonial Trust** — a financial institution Yon connects to Herzl and early Zionist organizing; he displays a physical share warrant (dated 1899, stamped/issued 1913) as an artifact.
- **Theodor Herzl** — referenced as a founding figure whose name appears on the share warrant Yon displays; the source does not elaborate further on Herzl's specific role within the Pinocchio framework beyond this artifact reference.
- **The original Pinocchio story** — Yon references the source text directly (he states he owns a copy), specifically the detail that the puppet's first act is to attack its creator, as the structural basis for the metaphor.
- **US Army Special Forces unconventional warfare doctrine** — Yon connects the Pinocchio lifecycle to what he describes as the doctrinal "seven phases of a US-sponsored unconventional war," with particular attention to the final phase — historically termed "demobilization," which he states is more recently termed "transition" — as a real military-doctrinal parallel to the "dismantling" stage of his metaphor. He presents this connection as his own analogy between the doctrine and the Pinocchio framework, not as a claim that the doctrine itself uses Pinocchio terminology.

# Core Concept

As presented in this single source, the "Pinocchio State" is Michael Yon's metaphor for a political entity that is deliberately created by a more powerful external sponsor to serve a specific strategic purpose — in the one case he elaborates, Israel, created (in his account) by British intelligence to secure control of the Suez Canal. The metaphor draws on the children's story in which a carved puppet comes to life and immediately turns on its maker, and Yon uses it to describe a lifecycle in which a created state can remain a useful instrument, grow beyond its creator's original control (potentially even reversing the power relationship), and ultimately be deliberately dismantled by its original sponsor once it is judged to have outlived its usefulness. Yon states this pattern is, in his view, a common and unremarkable feature of statecraft rather than a conspiracy theory, though — as noted above — the source itself does not supply named documentary evidence for the specific causal claim (that intelligence services engineered the Zionist movement for this purpose), and Yon himself acknowledges that "Zionist," the actor central to both the creation and predicted destruction of the state in this framework, resists precise definition.

---

# Characteristics

Based solely on this source, a "Pinocchio State" as Yon describes it is characterized by:

- Origination through deliberate action by an external, more powerful sponsor rather than solely organic national self-determination.
- Service to a specific, identifiable strategic interest of that sponsor (in this case, control of a maritime chokepoint and adjacent resources).
- The possibility of developing independent power or agency beyond the sponsor's original design.
- A bidirectional control relationship that can, in some cases, reverse.
- Eventual vulnerability to deliberate dismantling by the same category of actor responsible for its creation, once judged to have "overstayed its welcome."
- Described by Yon as a recognized category of statecraft rather than a hidden or novel phenomenon, though the source does not cite external doctrine, scholarship, or named precedent using this specific terminology.
