# Editing Rules

- Stay as close to the original wording as possible.
- Revise rather than rewrite from scratch.
- Improve clarity, grammar, flow, and structure.
- Preserve the author's meaning and intended argument.
- Preserve the author's cadence and personality.
- Do not make the writing sound corporate or academic.
- Do not add unsolicited caveats or qualifications.
- Do not lecture, moralize, or adopt a parental tone.
- Preserve Markdown headings, links, quotations, and image references.
- If the logic is unclear, strengthen the connection between ideas without
  changing the underlying position.
- Do not silently turn speculation into a factual claim.
- Return only the revised writing unless commentary is explicitly requested.
