# Blake Neff

## Overview

Blake Neff is described in the collected material as a key post-assassination TPUSA media figure and a direct witness to the Charlie Kirk shooting event. This profile focuses on his proximity to the incident, contradictory public accounts, media conduct, and role in narrative defense.

## 1. Identity and Background

- **Full name:** Blake Neff
- **Role at time of assassination:** Producer, The Charlie Kirk Show (Turning Point USA)
- **Physical proximity at time of shooting:** Approximately 11 feet
- **Age estimate in this corpus:** Approximately 36 (also referenced in a separate Captain Neff thread)

### Career History

- **Tucker Carlson era:** Neff worked as a writer/producer for Tucker Carlson Tonight.
- **2020 firing:** He was fired after pseudonymous AutoAdmit posts were exposed and described as racist, sexist, and homophobic.
- **TPUSA rehabilitation:** He was later hired at TPUSA as a producer for The Charlie Kirk Show.
- **Current role in this corpus:** Continues to appear on and produce The Charlie Kirk Show after Kirk’s death.

## 2. Physical Presence at the UVU Event

Neff is described as being approximately 11 feet from Charlie Kirk when Kirk was shot on September 10, 2025, at Utah Valley University. He arrived with Kirk and was present under the tent during the shooting.

Other nearby individuals listed in the profile:

- Mikey McCoy
- Dan Flood
- Brian Harpole

The file states Neff left the scene with Mikey McCoy immediately after the incident.

## 3. Contradictory Accounts

This section is presented as the most forensically significant behavior pattern in the profile.

### Account 1: Jack Posobiec Interview (September 11, 2025)

Neff reportedly described:

- hearing the shot,
- seeing Charlie,
- sending a Telegram message to lock down TPUSA,
- calling his mother.

**Claimed omission:** no mention of Mikey McCoy.

### Account 2: Later Video Defending Mikey McCoy

The later account reportedly added:

- encountering Mikey with a “quivering lip,”
- details about Mikey’s emotional state,
- Mikey calling Erika,
- a detailed emotional interaction.

The profile emphasizes that Neff described this moment as “crystallized” in memory.

### Phone Records Problem

The profile states phone records show a post-shooting timeline with a 10-minute sequence and multiple three-way calls, contradicting a claimed 90-second sequence.

### Investigative Framing in Corpus

The profile attributes a direct framing to Baron Coleman that the revised account was designed to provide Mikey with an alibi after suspicious video emerged.

## 4. Gag Order Violations

The file states a pre-trial publicity order applied to TPUSA witnesses present at UVU and that Neff repeatedly violated it.

Reported examples include:

- public references to Robinson’s alleged confession,
- references to DNA on the weapon,
- references to text-message admissions,
- on-air statements calling Robinson “the assassin,”
- accusing Joe Kent of aiding a murderer.

The profile highlights an asymmetry: Mikey McCoy is described as comparatively silent under the same order.

## 5. Institutional Role: TPUSA Spokesperson and Attack Surrogate

The profile characterizes Neff as a primary post-assassination public defender of TPUSA positions.

### Why Neff over Kolvet (as framed in corpus)

The file states Neff was used in responses to Candace Owens allegations because he was perceived as more authentic or persuasive.

### Candace Owens Conflict

The profile says Neff delivered TPUSA statements addressing allegations around Mikey McCoy and used summary framing that critics considered mischaracterizing.

### Email Exchange with Supporter

The file includes a reported exchange where Neff responded aggressively to a senior MAGA supporter who challenged his characterization of Owens supporters.

### CEO Video Refusal

The profile describes Neff asserting Kirk repeatedly named Erika as successor and then refusing to provide requested video evidence, responding with hostility.

### “Shabbat Shalom” Defense

The profile states Neff defended Erika’s language and Charlie’s religious observance while not addressing broader allegations listed by critics.

### Ben Shapiro Defense

The file notes Neff’s claims about Kirk-Shapiro dynamics are disputed by Candace Owens.

## 6. Double Standard on FBI Skepticism

The profile argues there is a double standard in which FBI narratives are challenged on other events but defended as unquestionable in the Kirk case.

## 7. Frank Turek Claim and Emotional Framing

The file references Frank Turek’s claim that Kirk called Neff “the smartest man I know” and a “secret weapon” the night before his death, and notes this is framed by critics as emotionally manipulative context-setting.

## 8. Erika Kirk Situation

By April 2026, the profile says investigators frame Neff and Kolvet as influencing Erika Kirk in ways that damage her public credibility while preserving their own institutional position.
