The whole vaccine story reminds me of the lack of fear people have when flying on an airplane. For example, if a 4-year-old child were to fly for the first time, that child might be nervous and excited, maybe a little scared, but ultimately calm and quiet.

If someone from 200 years ago experienced the same thing without context, they might have a heart attack and think they were about to die a horrible death. We take it for granted how normal it is to fly.

The difference isn’t the physic but the social context. The child isn’t afraid because everyone around him is calm. The adults act like everything is normal because they’ve read the statistics and they’ve flown dozens of times before. The child takes solace in their resolve.

Public trust works the same way. People feel calm when they believe a system has a long safety record and when the signals around them are consistent. When something new is introduced under the umbrella of that trusted system (e.g. vaccines), the existing confidence often carries over automatically. This is where mRNA tech comes in, it was introduced under the familiar banner of “vaccines” with all it’s percceived safety track record.

It didn’t arrive as something radically new (which it absolutely fucking is) it was dressed as a safe and effective intervention. It’s like boarding what you thought was a normal airplane, but all the parts are different and there is no pilot.

Maybe the aircraft is safe. Maybe it isn’t. But you never consented to finding out nor does anyone feel inclined on letting you know either. In fact, days, weeks, and/or months leading up to your perceived flight, the friendly guy on the television, your usual trusted engineer Anthony Couchi, has been warning you of crazies out there, that don’t believe in science and think crazy stuff like all airplanes are dangerous. You might even see one yourself someday, one of these crazies that don’t believe in science Obviously.

But all is well, passenger. Just board the “airplane” as usual and ignore those crazies. In fact, those crazies will likely go away someday, likely earn some darwinian award, because let’s face it, they aren’t cut from the same cloth. So be at ease friend. Worry not and enjoy your flight
