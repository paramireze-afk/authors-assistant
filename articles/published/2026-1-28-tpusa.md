Why the Left Should Be Paying Attention to the TPUSA Story
Paul Ramirez
Jan 28, 2026
If I were a Democrat who genuinely wanted to hurt Trump and the modern Republican machine, I would be obsessed with the Charlie Kirk and Turning Point USA story.

It has embedded itself deeply into Republican infrastructure, college campuses, donor networks, faith organizations, media organizations, and even elements of the Trump campaign ecosystem.

TPUSA is conducting mass layoffs because of leaks coming out. Those who were fired and did not leak to Candace, are turning around and becoming whistleblowers. Independent journalists, including Wolves and Finance, have documented what appear to be serious financial irregularities, including missing employees, missing millions of dollars, and questionable tax filings. Some of these allegations intersect with 2024 Trump campaign activity. Whether every claim holds up or not is beside the point.

Then there’s the rhetoric coming from insiders themselves.

It is not normal for people associated with a political organization to publicly say things like “they didn’t kill themselves.” It is not normal for people to openly express fear for their own safety on air. When an RNC committeewoman goes public claiming extensive foreign intelligence involvement (which Joe Kent was told specifically to not investigate by Kash Petal) and says she doesn’t know how she’s still alive.

Then there are reportings about how TPUSA Faith have been taking over religious institutions, shaping political theology in real time. It has cultivated loyalty structures that resemble ideological grooming more than civic engagement. And it has placed itself at the center of Republican youth politics with almost no serious adversarial coverage.

Instead, attention gets diverted to endless rage bait. ICE. Culture war skirmishes. The same “Orange man bad” loop that no longer persuades anyone who wasn’t already convinced. Meanwhile, an organization that may represent one of the most vulnerable pressure points in the modern Republican ecosystem is unraveling in a hilarioius, real time way view.

Even critics of religion should be paying attention. It appears to represent a deliberate effort to fuse political power with religious authority. When you see pastors around the country all condemning Candace Owens and Tucker Carlson, as a moral authority, this is precisely where your attention should be.

The thing I want to avoid is stop making people like Anthony Fauci a hero and then make people like Aaron Rodgers appear to be what is wrong with society.

If the left wants to be effective rather than reactive, curious rather than performative, this is exactly the kind of scandal it should be willing to examine in real time. Not because it confirms existing narratives, but because it challenges them.

And that’s where real leverage lives.
