# Did You Know There Was a Major Battle Taking Place at Cornell Leading Up to the Cornell 7 Headlines?

*College campuses are the front lines of the free speech battle*

You may not have heard, but there was recently an anti-war event at Cornell, one critics called anti-Israel, that took place days before the Cornell 7 story hit all the major news headlines. I didn't know about it either. I only found out because I was watching the DarkHorse Podcast, Bret and Heather were discussing the Cornell 7, and I kept asking myself the same question: why is this national news?

The answer I found had less to do with what happened in a fraternity house two years ago and more to do with what was happening at Cornell right now.

## The Event You Didn't Hear About

In mid-September, a student coalition called Students Not Bombs invited Hasan Piker, one of the most popular and controversial political streamers in the country, to speak on campus. The coalition is pushing Cornell to divest from Israel and its wars. Meanwhile, a former student known as Jane Doe filed a lawsuit over an alleged gang rape at a fraternity house back in 2024, the case now known as the Cornell 7. Look at the timing:

- **September 15:** Students Not Bombs announces Piker's visit. Tickets sell out within hours.
- **September 16:** Jane Doe files a civil lawsuit.
- **September 24:** Piker speaks at Sage Chapel.
- **September 28:** The DA reopens the case, citing the "community outrage stemming from the incomplete news and social media coverage."
- **October 2:** Governor Kathy Hochul appoints Attorney General Letitia James as special prosecutor.

## What I Think the Actual Story Is

It seems to me that the Cornell 7 story has been weaponized to turn the public's attention into emotional outrage over a two-year-old case, one the DA didn't think was worth investigating until "community outrage stemming from the incomplete news and social media coverage" forced his hand. So to me, the actual story is: who is the Goliath here? On one side, you have students at a college campus wanting to express their opposition to the foreign wars taking place, among other things. On the other, you have large swaths of the media apparatus seemingly moving in lockstep, all running with the same punchy tagline: "Cornell 7."

## Are We Falling for It Again?

I think a lot of people are missing the underlying battle taking place at Cornell. By narrowly focusing public outrage on a contested two-year-old case, the media is giving powerful interests leverage to pressure the university into silencing dissenting voices on Israel's actions in the Middle East.
