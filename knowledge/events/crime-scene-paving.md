# The Paving of the UVU Crime Scene

## What This Document Is

This is a research reference on the paving of the crime scene at Utah Valley University that occurred on September 14, 2025, four days after Charlie Kirk's assassination. The document presents investigative claims, source attributions, and competing explanations. Claims are labeled by evidentiary status where information is contested or uncertain.

## Event Timeline

### September 9, 2025 — Kirk Assassinated at UVU

Charlie Kirk is killed in the outdoor amphitheater courtyard at approximately 12:23 PM. The area becomes an active federal crime scene. [18]

### September 9, 2025 — Camera Removed from Above Kirk's Position

Within minutes of the shooting, a TPUSA employee removes the SD card from a camera positioned directly above where Kirk had been sitting. [17]

### September 11, 2025 — FBI Reportedly Orders Local Police Not to Interview Eyewitnesses

James Li reports that the FBI shut down local law enforcement participation in witness interviews around this period. [9]

### September 13, 2025 — Crime Scene Paved Over

On a Sunday, four days after the assassination, DFCM pushes through a $36,000-plus paving project. Soil is excavated and concrete pavers are installed over the exact location where Kirk was killed. No contracts or bids exist according to DFCM's own GRAMA response. [18][2][5]

### September 13, 2025 — Potted Plants Placed Over Paved Area

After paving is completed, numerous potted plants are placed around the area. A large pot is positioned directly over Kirk's former position. [6][7]

### September 30, 2025 — FBI Reportedly Wrapping up Investigation

James Li reports the FBI is closing its investigation despite numerous unanswered questions. Stage area, tunnel access, and planter boxes are also being dismantled. [9]

## Background

On Wednesday, September 10, 2025, Charlie Kirk was killed at Utah Valley University while speaking at a student event in an outdoor amphitheater courtyard. The area where he was seated became an active crime scene under federal investigation.

Four days later, on Sunday, September 14, that courtyard was paved over with concrete pavers. The grass where Kirk had been sitting was removed, soil was excavated, and hardscape was installed. Potted plants were subsequently placed around the area, including a large pot positioned directly over the spot where Kirk had been seated.

For independent investigators, this is one of the most significant and least-explained facts in the entire case. For those who accept the official narrative, it has been characterized as a pre-scheduled maintenance project that happened to coincide with the assassination.

## Who Ordered the Paving

### The Official Position

Utah's Division of Facilities Construction Management (DFCM), a state-level agency, pushed through the paving project. Utah Valley University itself was reportedly not the initiating party.

A commercial roofer who works at UVU and was interviewed by researcher Jason Goodman confirmed that UVU was confused by the operation and that DFCM drove it, not the university. [2] This account has not been independently corroborated by a second source.

### What the Records Show

In response to a GRAMA request (Utah's public records law), DFCM stated in writing that there were no contracts and no bids associated with this project, and that those records were not provided because they do not exist. [18]

The records that were provided tell a different story about the scale of the work. The paperwork shows site preparation, paver installation, concrete work, fencing, pressure washing, road base delivery, forklift rental, and material purchases, all tied to the same short time window. The total authorized amount exceeded $36,000. [18]

Instead of a normal procurement trail, the file consists primarily of same-day retail receipts from Lowe's, invoices from local suppliers and rental companies, and a contractor invoice issued after the work was already completed. Materials were purchased off the shelf as needed rather than ordered through a contracted vendor. [18]

What is absent is what would normally be expected for a project of this size: bid solicitations, competing quotes, an awarded contract, or documentation explaining why competitive bidding was skipped. For state-funded hardscape and site work of this scale, planning and procurement are typically done in advance. [18]

### Named Individuals

Four DFCM officials have been identified by Baron Coleman as allegedly involved in authorizing or executing the project: [5]

- Andy Mar, Director of DFCM
- Matt Bowyer, Deputy Director
- Guy Wayman, Capital Improvements Manager
- Tim Christensen, Capital Improvements Statewide Paving Program Manager

All four reportedly failed to respond to inquiries. Their cell phone numbers were obtained by Coleman, who sent texts to each, but he has not published those numbers. Inclusion of these names in the investigation does not constitute evidence of wrongdoing. They are identified as the officials responsible for a state project whose authorization trail is incomplete.

## Was This Project Pre-Scheduled?

This is the central factual dispute.

The official or semi-official explanation offered has been that the paving was a pre-scheduled maintenance project that happened to coincide with the assassination. Investigators dispute this on two grounds.

First, the project does not appear in UVU's approved fiscal year 2026 capital improvement list. [18] If it had been planned in advance, it would typically appear in that budget documentation.

Second, leaked text messages cited by Brandi and Billie reportedly showed the project was rushed and needed to be completed quickly. [1] The provenance and authenticity of those text messages has not been independently verified, and this claim should be treated as reported but unconfirmed.

Even if the project had been pre-scheduled, investigators argue that the area was now an active crime scene and that any pre-existing maintenance work should have been postponed to preserve physical evidence. [1] This is a procedural argument that does not depend on the pre-scheduling question being resolved either way.

## What Was Physically Destroyed

The paving process involved excavating existing soil. Baron Coleman, citing the work of researcher Hardscape Utah, noted that the process involves digging out existing soil to a depth sufficient for the base layer, which would remove any physical evidence present in the ground including blood, device fragments, ammunition fragments, or pieces of jewelry. [3]

The FBI was reportedly wrapping up its investigation around this same period. Separately, reporting from James Li noted that the FBI allegedly ordered local police not to interview eyewitnesses, and that the crime scene was being actively dismantled and rebuilt, including the stage area, tunnel to backstage, and planter boxes around the quad, within weeks of the assassination. [9]

A camera that had been positioned directly above where Kirk was sitting was also removed from the scene within minutes of the shooting, before the paving occurred. This is a separate event but part of the same pattern of physical evidence removal. [17]

## The Potted Plants

After the paving was completed, numerous potted plants were placed around the area. A large pot was positioned directly over the location where Kirk had been seated. [6]

Brandi and Billie reported a theory that certain plants can mask the scent of explosive residue, potentially deterring bomb-sniffing dogs from detecting evidence. [6] This specific claim, that the plants placed were of a variety known to interfere with canine detection, has not been verified by any publicly identifiable source. It should be treated as speculative.

The placement of potted plants at a college campus is not inherently unusual. Whether the specific placement pattern here is unusual is a matter of interpretation.

## Why Investigators Consider This Significant

### The Explosive Residue Theory

The most developed investigative theory holds that the paving was necessary not because of a shooting, but because something exploded at or near Kirk's position. If an explosive device was used, residue would remain in the soil. Cyclonite (RDX), a military-grade explosive compound, breaks down slowly after detonation and its residue can persist in soil as dangerous particles. [8]

In 2019, AES (a company with connections to other threads in this investigation) was fined for improper storage of cyclonite, which caused workers to suffer seizures. [8] Investigators have drawn a connection between AES and the Kirk assassination through separate threads not covered in this document.

The theory holds that removing approximately six inches of soil in every direction from the detonation point, then covering it with pavement, would eliminate both the forensic evidence of an explosion and the health risk to students from residue exposure. [4] The placement of plants would then serve as an additional layer of scent masking.

Baron Coleman stated directly: "A shooting with a .30-06 rifle would not necessitate such a coverup, but explosive residue in the soil would." [4]

### The Pattern Argument

James Li has placed the UVU paving in a broader historical pattern. In the JFK assassination, the presidential limousine was scrubbed. In the RFK assassination, pantry walls were torn down. In the MLK assassination, Memphis Public Works employees cut down bushes at the crime scene the morning after the shooting, destroying a possible secondary shooter position. [10] Li argues that in each case, a lone gunman narrative was established alongside the rapid destruction of physical evidence that might have supported alternative theories. [10]

This is a pattern argument, not direct evidence. Its persuasive weight depends on whether the reader finds the historical parallels meaningful or coincidental.

### The Procurement Anomaly

Setting aside all theories about why the paving occurred, the procurement record is anomalous on its own terms. A $36,000-plus state construction project with no contracts, no bids, and no competitive procurement documentation, executed with same-day retail receipts and a retroactive contractor invoice, does not conform to standard state procurement practice. [18] This is a factual claim grounded in the GRAMA response documents, not in theory.

## Competing Explanations

**Pre-scheduled maintenance:** The paving was already planned and the timing was coincidental. This explanation is weakened by the absence of the project from UVU's capital improvement list and the absence of any pre-existing contract documentation.

**Routine response to a damaged crime scene:** Authorities may have determined the physical evidence they needed had already been collected and authorized the paving to restore the campus. This is plausible but has not been stated officially by any named authority.

**Evidence destruction to conceal a shooting:** The paving destroyed blood, bullet fragments, or other ballistic evidence that might have contradicted the official account of the shooting.

**Evidence destruction to conceal an explosion:** The paving removed explosive residue that would have indicated a device rather than a rifle was the cause of death.

No named official has provided an on-record explanation for why the paving proceeded on the timeline it did, why competitive procurement was bypassed, or who specifically authorized the project.

## Disagreements Among Investigators

Independent investigators broadly agree that the paving was suspicious and premature. The disagreement is about what it was concealing.

Coleman and the Brandi and Billie show have emphasized the explosive residue theory, connecting it to the cyclonite thread and the AES investigation. [4]

James Li has framed it primarily as a pattern consistent with historical political assassinations, without committing to a specific mechanism. [10]

Candace Owens has noted the paving as a definitive fact in the case while focusing her investigative energy on other threads, including the tunnel network beneath the UVU amphitheater. [11]

Baron Coleman's episode 84 lists the crime scene paving as one of the "definitive facts" in the case, distinguishing it from inferential claims. [12]

## What Is Well Supported

- The paving occurred on Sunday, September 14, 2025, four days after the assassination. This is confirmed by multiple independent sources and photographic evidence. [1]
- DFCM, not UVU, drove the project. UVU personnel were reportedly confused by it. [2]
- The project does not appear in UVU's approved fiscal year 2026 capital improvement list. [18]
- DFCM's own GRAMA response states that no contracts or bids exist for the project. [18]
- The project involved soil excavation, paver installation, and associated work totaling over $36,000. [18]
- The procurement record consists primarily of same-day retail receipts and a retroactive contractor invoice. [18]
- Potted plants were placed over the paved area after completion. [6]
- Four DFCM officials have been identified as associated with the project and have not responded to inquiries. [5]
- The paving is listed by Baron Coleman as a "definitive fact" in the case, distinct from inferential claims. [12]

## What Remains Uncertain

- Whether the project was genuinely pre-scheduled. No pre-existing documentation has been produced to support this claim, but absence of documentation is not the same as proof it was not planned.
- Who specifically authorized the project and on what basis competitive procurement was bypassed.
- Whether the leaked text messages cited by Brandi and Billie showing urgency are authentic. No independent verification of those messages has been reported. [1]
- Whether explosive residue was present in the soil. No soil samples were taken by independent investigators before the paving, and none have been reported from law enforcement.
- Whether the specific plants placed are of a variety that interferes with canine scent detection. This claim has not been verified by any publicly identifiable source. [6]
- Whether the FBI's reported instruction to local police not to interview witnesses is accurate. This comes from a single reporting source. [9]
- Whether the paving was coordinated with other evidence removal (camera SD card, stage demolition) or whether those events are coincidental.
