# Tyler Bowyer

## At a Glance

Tyler Bowyer is a longtime Arizona Republican operative who became one of the most powerful figures inside Turning Point USA without ever being its most visible one. He served as Chief Operating Officer of TPUSA and later of affiliated entities, and is described by investigators as central to infrastructure, staffing, and financial-routing systems around Charlie Kirk.

## Network Summary

His significance to the Kirk assassination investigation is framed as structural rather than direct: investigators focus on patterns of financial irregularities, organizational control, and network relationships that they argue Kirk may have been examining in the days before his death.

Candace Owens has described Bowyer as the “linchpin” who staffed nearly everyone around Charlie Kirk [6].

Who He Is (One-Paragraph Summary)
Tyler Bowyer is a longtime Arizona Republican operative who became one of the most powerful figures inside Turning Point USA without ever being its most visible one. He served as Chief Operating Officer of TPUSA and later of its affiliated entities, and is widely described by investigators and former insiders as the person who built the organizational infrastructure around Charlie Kirk, including hiring most of the staff, relocating headquarters to Arizona, and managing the network of shell companies through which hundreds of millions of dollars flowed. His significance to the Kirk assassination investigation lies not in any direct connection to the shooting, but in the pattern of financial irregularities, organizational control, and network relationships that investigators believe Kirk was beginning to uncover in the days before his death.

Background
Grew up in the Chandler/Gilbert area of Arizona, part of a tightly networked Mormon community
Majored in Soviet studies and is a fluent Russian speaker
Has documented involvement with orphanages in Ukraine prior to his TPUSA career
Was involved in Maricopa County Republican politics before joining TPUSA, eventually becoming chairman of the Maricopa County Republican Committee
His family has generational ties to the Farnsworth family of Arizona, a prominent Mormon political dynasty: Bowyer and Tarrell Farnsworth attended Gilbert High School, their parents attended school together in Chandler, and Tarrell’s father Dwayne Farnsworth is first cousins with longtime Arizona Congressman Eddie Farnsworth and Senator David Farnsworth [15]
While still in college, Bowyer arranged a trip to meet with Romanian Parliament members, a detail investigators find notable given Romania’s proximity to U.S. military base activity and his later Ukraine connections [13]

Organizational Roles and Structure
Bowyer’s influence operated across multiple entities simultaneously, which is central to understanding why investigators focus on him.

The Four TPUSA Entities
| Entity | Type | Bowyer’s Role | |---|---|---| | Turning Point USA | 501(c)(3) | COO until 2022 (disputed), then informal | | Turning Point Action | 501(c)(4) | COO (official, from 2022) | | America’s Turning Point | 501(c)(3) | Managed directly by Bowyer | | Turning Point Endowment | 501(c)(3) | Connected via board overlap |

The key structural point: America’s Turning Point existed as a separate 501(c)(3) managed by Bowyer, not Kirk. Investigators describe this as giving Bowyer “his own 501 where he could do whatever he wanted and Charlie Kirk would not necessarily know about it” [11]. It received $8.6 million in grants transferred from TPUSA in the months before Kirk’s death, yet reported zero employees on its 990 form [10].

The Independent Contractor Arrangement
Bowyer was listed as an independent contractor rather than an employee on TPUSA’s 990 forms, despite functioning as COO for approximately a decade. IRS conflict-of-interest rules that apply to officers and directors do not apply to independent contractors. This is significant because Bowyer simultaneously served on the board of Super Feed Technologies alongside Erika Kirk’s mother Lori Frantzve, while TPUSA was doing business with Super Feed. The contractor classification may have been used specifically to avoid mandatory conflict-of-interest disclosures [4].

Financial Controversies
The 110 LLC Payments
Turning Point entities under Bowyer’s management sent a total of $4.7 million to a company called 110 LLC, whose registered address is a UPS store mailbox. The breakdown:

Turning Point Action: $1.1 million (2021-22 cycle), $2.1 million (2023-24 cycle)
Additional transfers from other TPUSA entities
Bowyer has not explained what 110 LLC is or what services it provided. Arizona Attorney General Kris Mayes has issued no subpoena and made no public statement about this [8].

The Donor Surge
Donations to Turning Point Action increased from $10.7 million in FY23 to $27.2 million in FY24, a jump of $16.5 million. TPUSA’s lawyer, in a letter responding to investigators, described this as spending on “lobbying and political activity during election years.” This directly contradicts the organization’s own 990 form, which reported only 3% political activity [10]. Investigators have raised the question of whether Bowyer was redirecting major donors away from TPUSA proper and toward entities he controlled.

The Missing 990 Forms
On May 15, 2025, TPUSA filed only one of its four annual 990 forms. The forms for Turning Point Endowment, Turning Point Action, and America’s Turning Point were never submitted [21]. The October 15 extension deadline was also missed [23]. This prevented public scrutiny of the $8.6 million America’s Turning Point transfer. Baker Tilly, TPUSA’s auditor, issued a clean audit opinion without flagging the missing filings.

The Kirk Audit Memo
On September 2, 2025, eight days before his assassination, Charlie Kirk issued an internal memo appointing a new COO (Justin Streiff) and ordering a DOGE-style audit, described as a second independent audit on top of the standard annual financial audit [22]. Investigators note this is a typical corporate response to discovering financial misconduct. After Kirk’s death, the audit was cancelled. Bowyer publicly responded that no audit was necessary because TPUSA already audits annually, which mischaracterizes what Kirk had ordered.

Key Relationships
Charlie Kirk
Bowyer described himself as Kirk’s best friend and referred to the two of them (along with Candace Owens) as “the three amigos.” He claims to have been present when Owens met Kanye West. He is credited with convincing Kirk to relocate TPUSA headquarters from Chicago to Arizona. Candace Owens, however, states she does not trust Bowyer and that “he makes my skin crawl” [1]. The relationship is significant because Bowyer effectively built the organizational world around Kirk, controlling hiring, operations, and the Arizona political network, while Kirk was the public face.

Erika Kirk (nee Frantzve)
Bowyer introduced Erika Frantzve to Charlie Kirk. Erika cannot or will not explain how she knows Bowyer [2]. Bowyer has stated that Erika was “ultimately the reason” TPUSA moved to Arizona, suggesting she was involved in that decision before her relationship with Charlie was public. Investigators note that Bowyer, Erika, and Erika’s mother Lori Frantzve now appear to be operating in alignment at TPUSA following Kirk’s death, with Bowyer telling people about a new real estate venture he claims was Kirk’s vision [17].

The Farnsworth Network
The Farnsworth connection is one of the more structurally significant relationships in the investigation. Bowyer has generational family ties to the Farnsworths [15]. Tarrell Farnsworth is the IT contractor who was present at the UVU event where Kirk was killed and who investigators allege moved a chair to access SD cards and filmed a selfie video immediately after the shooting [9]. Duane Farnsworth (Tarrell’s father) shares a last name and family connection with the founders of a 2017 company called We the People USA, Inc., whose directors included Bowyer, Jake Hoffman, and current TPUSA board members Mike Miller and Doug DeGroote [9].

Crystal Clanton
Clanton was TPUSA’s national field director and was described by Kirk himself as the effective number two at the organization. Her departure in 2017 following a scandal directly preceded Bowyer’s elevation to COO. Investigators note that Bowyer was at the very bottom of TPUSA’s organizational chart in October 2016 and at the top (alongside only Kirk and Jake Hoffman) by February 2018, two months after the Clanton story broke [3]. The question of who benefited from Clanton’s exit points directly to Bowyer.

Jake Hoffman
Hoffman appears alongside Bowyer in multiple organizational contexts: the 2017 We the People USA company, the post-Clanton TPUSA website, and Arizona Republican politics. The pattern of Bowyer and Hoffman doing side businesses together predates and runs parallel to their TPUSA roles [9].

Austin Smith
A former Arizona Republican lawmaker who worked for Turning Point Action and was later sentenced for using forged signatures on nominating petitions, including a dead woman’s signature. Owens reports receiving tips that Bowyer offered political favors in exchange for jobs at TPUSA [6]. Investigators treat Smith as an example of the broader pattern of Bowyer connecting TPUSA’s nonprofit status to Arizona political operations.

Public Statements and Behavioral Patterns
Several of Bowyer’s public statements have been flagged by investigators for inconsistency:

On his TPUSA role: Claims he has not been COO of TPUSA since July 2022. A Washington Post recording from late 2022 captures him saying “you’re talking to the guy that basically runs everything” specifically about TPUSA [5].
On the Kirk audit: Responded publicly that no special audit was needed, characterizing Kirk’s DOGE-style audit as equivalent to the standard annual audit. Investigators say this misrepresents what Kirk had ordered.
On meeting Erika Kirk: Gave a “both are true” answer when asked about contradictory accounts of when he and Erika first met, claiming they “met by phone” before meeting in person [16].
On transparency: Described himself as “a transparent piece of glass” on everything he does, while declining to explain the $4.7 million in payments to a UPS store mailbox [5].
On running TPUSA like a military operation: Tweeted in 2018 that the organization runs “everything like a military op” and that “all of our people are from that background” [12].
Lesser-Known Context
Bowyer’s academic background in Soviet studies and fluency in Russian are unusual for a conservative political operative. Combined with his documented involvement with Ukrainian orphanages and his presence in Romania for parliamentary meetings while still in college, investigators have noted a pattern of Eastern European connections that predates his TPUSA career [7].
Multiple former TPUSA employees reportedly knew about Bowyer’s alleged embezzlement and inappropriate relationships with young male interns. One source told investigators: “everybody knows about Tyler Bowyer at Turning Point USA. It’s like an absurdity” [7].
After Kirk’s death, Bowyer hired his own younger brother (who had spent ten years in dental sales) at TPUSA [17].
Bowyer is now publicly pitching a real estate venture targeting young Christians, claiming it was Kirk’s vision. Investigators note there is no documented evidence Kirk ever proposed this [17].

Investigative Significance: Why Bowyer Matters
The case for Bowyer’s significance is structural, not circumstantial. He is not significant because of one suspicious act. He is significant because of the position he occupied: the person who built the organization around Kirk, controlled its finances through entities Kirk did not directly oversee, hired most of the people in Kirk’s inner circle, and was in the process of being audited and removed from power at the moment Kirk was killed.

The pattern investigators have identified is: Bowyer built a parallel financial infrastructure inside TPUSA that Kirk did not control, Kirk discovered it, Kirk moved to expose it, and Kirk was killed before the audit could proceed. Whether or not that pattern reflects what actually happened, it is the organizing hypothesis that makes Bowyer a central figure in any serious investigation of the circumstances surrounding Kirk’s death.

Candace Owens has described Bowyer as the “linchpin” who staffed nearly everyone around Charlie Kirk [6]. That framing is worth holding onto: not a suspect in isolation, but a structural node through which most of the relevant relationships and financial flows pass.
